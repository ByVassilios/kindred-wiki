# Experience

Kindred earn experience (XP) into a pool and spend it on the Kindred tab. A little comes in every
night on its own. Most of it comes from doing things. What it costs to spend is on the
[character sheet](Character-Sheet.md#experience).

Everything below is scaled by the experience slider in the mod settings, and nothing is earned
in torpor. A gain of 0.1 or more floats over the vampire as "+0.5 XP: a speech", and the XP line
on the Character page says how much it has been earning a night lately. Each Discipline's buy
button says how many nights the next dot is at that rate.

## Where it comes from

| Source | XP |
|---|---|
| A night | 0.3 |
| A careful feeding | 0.1 |
| Holding back a frenzy | 0.5 |
| An enemy beaten | 0.05 to 2, by how dangerous it was. A vampire counts double, an elder four times, a hunter with True Faith half again. Sending an enemy vampire into torpor counts. At most 3 a night |
| A court mission done | 1, 2, 4 or 6 by the size of its reward, to every colony vampire who was there |
| Presented at a new court | 1, once per court |
| An ambition met | 3, 5 or 7 |
| A Path deed | 0.1, at most two a night |
| A first time | 1, or 0.5 for a power's first use on an enemy |
| A lesson | 0.3, one a night |
| Facing the flame | 0.1 a success, at most 0.3 a night |
| Holding the floor | 0.5 to 1.5 |
| A secret from a captive | 0.5 each |
| Reading a treatise | 0.3 an hour, for ten hours |
| A clean hunting trip | 0.3 |
| Reading lore texts, a sire's lessons, diablerie | See [Relics and lore](Relics-and-Lore.md) and [Embrace, bonds and ghouls](Embrace-Bonds-and-Ghouls.md) |

## Ambitions

Each colony vampire works toward one goal at a time. At its first dusk, a letter offers three
it could take, drawn from its clan, its Path and its age, and at least one from the clan or Path
where one fits. The Character page shows the goal and how far along it is. Only what happens
after it is taken counts. A met ambition pays at the next dusk, and a new letter comes the night
after. There is no deadline and no failure. Drop one, or turn all three down, and another letter
comes in five days.

## Path deeds

Each morality pays for a few acts that suit it, the way it punishes sins. A Humanity vampire who
tends the sick or a Path of Night vampire who spreads fear earns 0.1 XP each time, up to two a
night. A deed another morality would reward pays nothing.

| Morality | Deeds that pay |
|---|---|
| Humanity | Tended a patient; Won a prisoner over; Held back the Beast |
| Path of Blood | Committed diablerie; Given a vampire Final Death |
| Path of the Bones | Watched someone die; Read a corpse with the Spirit's Touch |
| Path of Caine | Finished a lore text; Learned a ritual |
| Path of Cathari | Drunk a high from a vessel's blood; Charmed a mortal |
| Path of the Feral Heart | Brought down an animal with the bite; Slept in the earth |
| Path of Honorable Accord | Repaid a boon; Finished a court mission |
| Path of Lilith | Questioned a captive with pain; Fed without care |
| Path of Metamorphosis | Grown in a Discipline; Taken another shape |
| Path of Night | Sent a mortal running in fear |
| Path of Paradox | Given a vampire Final Death |
| Path of Power and the Inner Voice | Bent a mind with Dominate; Given a servant an order |
| Path of Typhon | Left a mortal craving the Kiss |

## Firsts

The first time a vampire does something, it learns from it and gains 1 XP: each ritual it casts,
its first childe, its first blood bond, the first hunter it kills, the first sunrise it lives
through outdoors, the first frenzy it rides, its first waking from torpor, the first Kindred blood
it drinks and the first relic it identifies. Every power pays 0.5 the first time it is used on an
enemy or wins a contest.

## Lessons

A vampire learns a Discipline from another colony vampire who has more dots in it. Press
**Take lessons** on the student's Actions page and pick a Discipline and a teacher. The
student studies at a table in the same room as the teacher, two hours a night under the
Research work type, so Research has to be turned on in its Work tab. The teacher carries on
with its own work.

- Each lesson pays 0.3 XP into the usual pool.
- A new Discipline from outside the clan takes three lessons before its first dot can be bought.
  The first lesson starts with a point of the teacher's blood, which counts as a drink toward a
  blood bond.
- Drinking another vampire's blood no longer teaches its Disciplines. Lore texts, sleepers and a
  court's teachers still do.

The rules are the same for every way a Discipline is learned: no dots past the generation's
limit, Koldunic Sorcery only for the Tzimisce, Sanguinus only for the Blood Brothers, Melpominee
no higher than the vampire's Artistic, and Assamite sorcerers pay half again for other blood
magic. A non-Gargoyle who reaches Visceratika 4 turns grey and stony.

**Ghouls** take lessons from their domitor the same way and earn 0.6 ghoul XP a lesson. A ghoul
can't learn Protean, Thaumaturgy or a Discipline only one clan has, unless its domitor's clan is
that clan.

## Treatises

A colony vampire with three or more dots in a Discipline gets **Write a treatise** on its
Actions page. It writes at a table under Research, eight hours a dot, and can stop and come
back to it. The treatise is a book named for its writer. Its quality comes from the writer's
Intellectual skill.

A vampire who has the Discipline at fewer dots than the writer earns 0.3 XP an hour from reading
it, for ten hours, once per treatise. It doesn't unlock the Discipline. Anyone else reads it as an
ordinary book. Treatises also turn up in the world, written at 3 to 5 dots.

## Facing the flame

Right-click a lit torch or campfire and pick **Face the flame**, under Kindred. The vampire sits
close and tests itself against its fear every hour with a Courage roll, against 5 for a torch
and 6 for a campfire. Each success pays 0.1 XP, up to 0.3 a night. A failure ends it and the
vampire backs off. A botch is a Rötschreck right beside the fire. It can't be done by day or when
the vampire is hungry enough to frenzy.

## Holding the floor

A vampire may make a speech once per gathering: at an Elysium, rant or esbat the colony hosts
(**Hold the floor** on the vampire), or once per caravan visit to a court (a button in the court
window). The roll is Charisma + Social against 7. A success pays 0.5 XP, a quarter more for each
success past the first up to 1.5, and a point of standing with the court (two on three
successes). A failure does nothing. A botch is a scandal at that court.

## Questioning captives

Set a captive Kindred, ghoul or Society hunter to **Question** in its prisoner tab. A colony
vampire warden questions it once a night: Manipulation + Social against the captive's
Willpower. The warden uses the best method it has. Dominate 2 makes it two easier, though not on
a captive of a lower generation. Presence 3 makes it one easier. Without either it uses pain,
also one easier, which hurts the captive and is a sin at Humanity 4.

Each success pays 0.5 XP and gives up a secret. A neonate or ghoul has one secret, an ancilla
or hunter two, an elder three, and a letter says when the captive has nothing left.

| Captive | What it may give up |
|---|---|
| Kindred | A torpor crypt; a court's weakness or a rival's scheme; a ritual it knows; its sire, on the lineage tree |
| Ghoul | Its domitor, on the lineage tree; a court |
| Hunter | The next hunter raid put back; the way into a hideout holding a stolen heart (three more days); the cell for a mission |

## Hunting trips

After a [hunting trip](Feeding.md#hunting-trips), one time in four a letter asks what to do
instead of the usual message:

- A local vampire claims the herd: owe it a trivial boon and feed, fight it (a win is 1 XP and its
  blood), or go hungry.
- A hunter takes notice: kill him, slip away, or let him follow.
- A mortal who liked the Kiss asks to come along, craving it.
- A stranger offers a rumour for a blood vial.

A clean hunt with no letter pays 0.3 XP.
