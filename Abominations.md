# Abominations

An Abomination is a Garou who rose from the Embrace. It needs both this mod and The Rim by Night:
Garou loaded; with either alone, none of this happens. The werewolves' side is on the Garou wiki's
[Abominations](https://github.com/ByVassilios/garou-wiki/blob/main/Abominations.md) page.

## How one is made

- A vampire Embraces a Garou. Before it dies, the Garou rolls Gnosis at difficulty 6. A success
  is a quiet death, a failure a death in agony, and only a botch rises. That is about 10% at
  Gnosis 1, 6% at 3, 2% at 5 and under 1% at 7. The confirmation shows the odds.
- An unwilling Garou with Willpower left, and not in Harano, spends it for a sure clean death.
  One that asked, or is willing, never does.
- It rises a few hours later as a vampire of its sire's clan, one generation weaker. It keeps its
  forms, Rage, Gnosis, Gifts, tribe, breed, auspice and rank.

## Ego

Humanity is replaced by Ego, the Hierarchy of Wyrm Taint. It starts at the Garou's Gnosis.

- Ego never rises, and experience can't buy it, the Virtues or Willpower.
- By day, Ego caps every dice pool.
- Below 7 it smells of the Wyrm: Sense Wyrm finds it and the Banes treat it as a friend.
- Its own ambition, the wolf's hunger, is to bring down five animals with the bite.
- At 0 it is the Wyrm's. It leaves to join the Black Spiral Dancers and comes back with their
  raids as a nemesis.

The sins on the list, worst first: breaking the Litany (10), feeding on a Garou or Kinfolk (9),
hurting a friend in a frenzy (8), calling on a Bane (7), killing in a frenzy (6), killing a
Garou, Kinfolk or wolf outside a fight it was attacked in (5), diablerie or torture (4), killing
an innocent (3), a Bane fetish or fighting beside fomori (2), helping destroy a caern (1).

## Living as one

| Rule | What happens |
|---|---|
| Dice pools | Every pool is halved, never below one die. A point of Willpower gives full pools until sunrise, by the "Full pools tonight" button, or on its own when a fight starts at night |
| Willpower | Buys no automatic success |
| One pool a turn | No blood within a turn of spending Rage or Gnosis, and the other way round |
| Healing | Only with blood. No regeneration |
| Gnosis | Never refills |
| Renown and rank | Frozen at the Embrace |
| Its blood | Dead: it can't Embrace or make ghouls |
| Silver | Burns it in every form, Homid included |
| Frenzy | Anger is the wolf's frenzy, rolled on Rage. Hunger frenzy rolls Rage too |
| Mood | "Dead and cut off from Gaia", -12, for good. A new break, "walk into the dawn", sends it out to stand under the sky |

Its Gifts that use only Rage, or nothing, work as before. A Gift that calls on spirits reaches
only Banes and tests Ego. About once a season a Bane offers a Gift of the Black Spiral Dancers,
bought with experience.

## Who hunts it

- **Courts:** a court that learns of it, because one of its vampires saw it shift or the colony
  visited with it, calls a blood hunt and demands it. Hand it over, or refuse for -15 goodwill
  while it lives and court raids aimed at it.
- **Septs:** a sept's Garou who sees it in a wolf's shape tells the sept: -20 goodwill while it
  lives, and the next raid comes for it.
- **Its own colony:** colony Garou think 20 less of it, colony Kindred 5 less.

## Other ways to meet one

- A colony Garou deep in Harano, or of a fallen tribe, may ask a colony vampire for the Embrace.
- A hunted Abomination may come in by night and ask for shelter. Take it in and the blood hunt
  and the sept's anger come with it. Turn it away and its hunters may pass through after it.
  Sometimes one comes to kill instead.
