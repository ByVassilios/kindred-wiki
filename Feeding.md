# Feeding

A hungry vampire finds blood by itself, the way a colonist finds food. You can also right-click a
vessel and pick "Feed". This page covers where the blood comes from and what it costs.

## Sources

| Source | Blood | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Willing colonists | Up to 3 points | Leaves mild blood loss that clears in about a day. The vessel gets the Kiss, a short pleasant daze |
| Prisoners | Up to 3 points | Feeding on someone who hasn't agreed is a sin at Humanity 8 |
| Sleeping or downed pawns | Up to 3 points | Only a Masquerade risk if an outsider sees |
| Animals | About half per point, scaled by size | Worth less from the 8th generation down, nothing from the 6th |
| Blood vials | 1 point each | Drawn at a medical bed. They keep four days, so store them in a freezer |
| Other Kindred | Full value | Each drink is a step toward a blood bond, and it can get a vampire hooked |
| Fresh corpses | Little | Costs Humanity |

Draining the last of a vessel's blood kills it. Careful feedings close their own wound. Frenzied
feeding and bites in a fight leave **fang marks**, which outsiders and hunters can read.

Children are never fed on, ghouled or Embraced.

## Feeding on a sleeper

A sleeping vessel may wake when the fangs go in. Each time, the feeder rolls Dexterity plus its
Obfuscate dots against 6 (it goes in the roll log). A drunk vessel makes it easier, and a greedy
feeding or a drain harder. Mortals who crave the Kiss, ghouls and the feeder's own thralls don't
mind and get no roll.

| Result | What happens |
|---|---|
| Success | It sleeps through and remembers nothing |
| Failure | It wakes, but the Kiss holds it until the feeding ends. It remembers, as if fed on awake, and an outsider is a witness. Dominate 3's forgetting still works |
| Botch | It wakes screaming. The feeding stops, it witnessed the bite, and everyone asleep nearby wakes too |

Anyone awake who sees the feeding is still a witness. [Tavern](Tavern.md#guests-as-vessels)
guests asleep in rented beds are the usual sleepers.

## Blood vials

- **Drawing blood** is a bill at a medical bed. The donor loses some blood, the colony gets a
  vial.
- **Grades.** A vial remembers its donor. Good looks, a sanguine temper, Tough and good mood raise
  the grade; ugliness, gloom, drug interest, bad health or bad mood lower it. Fine blood lifts the
  drinker's mood a little and thin blood dampens it. The points are the same.
- **What it carries.** A vial keeps its donor's drug high and disease. See below.
- **Blood wine.** A brewery bill turns five fine vials into five bottles. Wine keeps for 30 days
  and lifts the mood more. The AI drinks vials only, so give wine by order.
- **Kindred prisoners** are fed vials by wardens when the colony has them.

## The vessel pen

"Keep as vessel" is a prisoner interaction mode. Wardens bring food and nothing else: no
recruiting, no release. Hungry vampires pick from the pen by whoever has the least blood loss, so
the pen rotates by itself. Prisoners kept this way get a "kept as cattle" thought unless they've
come to like the Kindred. A long-held vessel can come round and be recruited later.

## What the blood carries

- **Drugs.** If the vessel is high, the vampire gets a share of the same high. It's the only way
  Kindred feel drugs. They never get tolerance or addiction from it.
- **Disease.** A sick vessel leaves the vampire carrying "tainted blood" for a few days. It shows
  in the health tab and has no symptoms, but each mortal fed on in that time may catch the
  disease.

## How mortals feel about it

Your mortal colonists know what their neighbours are. Each one has a **regard** for Kindred:

| Regard | Mood near Kindred | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Devoted | Good | Volunteers as a vessel, may ask to be ghouled or Embraced |
| Fascinated | Slightly good | Counts as a willing vessel |
| Uneasy | Slightly bad | The usual start |
| Fearful | Bad | Worse opinion of Kindred |
| Hateful | Very bad | Won't be fed on awake, and may try to kill a vampire |

Regard goes up with the Kiss, being saved by a vampire and long quiet stretches. It goes down
with unwilling feeding, a friend killed by a vampire, or seeing a frenzy. Traits push the
starting roll: psychopaths lean fascinated, kind pawns lean fearful.

A hateful mortal's mental break can turn into **"slay the monster"**: it goes after a vampire,
ideally one asleep by day, with fire or a stake. A warning thought shows well before this, so you
have time to exile, imprison, bond or win them over. A blood bond or ghouling sets regard to
devoted and keeps it there.

### Craving the Kiss

A mortal fed on often may come to crave it and become a **blood doll**. While fed regularly they
get a small mood bonus and offer themselves first. Without the Kiss they go into withdrawal for a
while, and the craving fades after about a quadrum. A doll wants feeding more often than is safe,
so set a blood-loss limit.

## Biting in combat

Every vampire has fangs as a melee attack. A bite on a standing enemy is unlikely; on a downed,
staked or held one it's the first choice. Each bite that lands drinks a point, up to three per
target per fight. Bites do aggravated damage to other Kindred. Frenzied vampires bite first.

## Hooked on Kindred vitae

Drinking another vampire's blood is a rush. Each time, the drinker rolls Self-Control. A failed
roll moves it toward needing more: a craving, then a habit, then a break where it goes looking
for Kindred blood. It fades with time away from it.

## On the road

- A caravan with any mortal or ghoul in it travels by day as usual. The Kindred ride covered.
- An all-Kindred caravan travels by night and rests through the day.
- A Gangrel in a caravan feeds its Kindred from game, so they need no blood that day.

### Hunting trips

A caravan with a Kindred can pick **Hunt** at any settlement that isn't hostile, once a night.
Choose **careful** (less blood, low risk) or **greedy** (fills the pool, real risk). The roll uses
Wits and Streetwise, plus the vampire's best of Obfuscate, Presence, Dominate and Animalism.

| Result | What happens |
|---|---|
| Good | Fed, nobody the wiser |
| Poor | Fed a little |
| Failed | One of: a witness, a dead vessel (a sin), or lost goodwill |
| Botched | Two of those, and a hunter comes looking later |

Hunting the same settlement again in a season gets harder. Hunting in a court's city without rank
there is poaching. On empty land a caravan can hunt animals safely for a little blood.

Now and then a hunt ends in a letter with a choice instead: a local vampire who claims the herd, a
hunter who noticed, a mortal who wants to come along, a stranger with news. See
[Experience](Experience.md#hunting-trips).
