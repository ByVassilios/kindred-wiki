# Werewolves

The werewolves come from The Rim by Night: Garou, the series' Werewolf: The Apocalypse mod. In the
game this page shows only when both mods are loaded. The werewolves' side of all this is on the
page "Kindred and Garou", in the Garou chapter of the grimoire and on the
[Garou wiki](https://github.com/ByVassilios/garou-wiki/blob/main/Kindred-and-Garou.md).

Garou call vampires leeches, and most of them count Kindred as creatures of the Wyrm, the
corruption they exist to fight. Expect them to attack on sight.

## Garou and the Embrace

- **Embracing a Garou** is offered, and the confirmation says what happens: the Garou is drained
  to death and never rises. A Garou never asks for the Embrace.
- **Ghouling a Garou** spends the vitae for nothing, and no blood bond takes one.
- **Kinfolk**, the Garou's human families, can be ghouled and Embraced. The Embrace ends the
  wolf blood in them for good.

## What they smell

A vampire below Humanity 7, or on a Path of Enlightenment, smells of the Wyrm to a Garou. Their
Gifts that hunt the Wyrm find it, hurt it more, and pay the Garou double experience for the kill.
A vampire with Humanity 7 or more reads as something dead, but not the Wyrm's.

## Fighting them

- A Garou's claws and bite are aggravated to a vampire. Fire is aggravated to both.
- Garou heal fast, so a fight with one is long. Silver hurts them; ordinary blades and bullets
  barely slow them down.
- Dominate and Presence work on a Garou, but not on one in a frenzy, and a Garou resists with
  Willpower plus Rage.
- The Garou's war form terrifies mortals. It doesn't touch vampires, except one Gift, Terror of
  the Dire Wolf.

Some Gifts are made for vampires: one burns the undead nearby with the light of the sun, some end a frenzy or force
one, one stops vitae from healing, one steals a Discipline, and the oldest Silver Fangs can undo
centuries of undeath at once.

## Drinking from a Garou

Each point of a Garou's blood gives two vitae. It also leaves the vampire on edge for a few days:
frenzy and Rötschreck are harder to resist, and they're quick to pick a fight. The Garou gains
Rage, and may frenzy.

## Sharing a colony

A colony can keep both, and it's tense. The Garou resent every vampire in it, more for one that
smells of the Wyrm, and roll for frenzy when a vampire of the colony feeds in sight. Vampires near
the colony's Garou are uneasy. The septs of the Garou Nation think less of the colony each day it
keeps both. Friendship between a vampire and a Garou can still grow, slowly.

## Events

- **A Garou pack hunt**: a pack of Garou gathers at the edge of a colony with vampires and no Garou.
  A vampire with Humanity 7 or more can go out and talk them down, from the letter: Charisma plus
  Social against the pack's best Willpower. Fail, and they attack at once.
- **The Black Spiral and Sabbat raid**: a rare raid by night of Garou who serve the Wyrm, with up to
  three Sabbat vampires among them.
- **Hunters** come armed for every kind the colony holds.

The Masquerade and the Garou's Veil are separate: a mortal who saw a vampire feed and a werewolf
change counts against both. These events answer to the setting for Kindred's attacks.

## Feuds

Each side names the colony's pawn who hurt its own, for two quadrums or until that pawn dies:

- A colony vampire who feeds on or kills kinfolk is named by the nearest sept. While they're on a
  home map, the Garou pack hunt comes three times as often, even to a colony with Garou of its own,
  and its letter names them.
- A colony werewolf who destroys a court's vampire, or the childe of one of its members, is named
  by that court. Its sect's raids come as often as in a blood hunt, and its stalkers strike at the
  named werewolf first.

In the background, septs and courts within 30 tiles of each other fight now and then: a sept's
raid destroys one of the court's vampires, never its ruler, or the court's ghouls strike the caern.
The court's chronicle says so.
