# Blood sorcery

Each blood sorcery has several **paths**, learned and raised separately like Disciplines of their
own, and a set of **rituals**.

## Paths

- **Primary path.** The first path learned. It can reach 5 (or higher, for an elder).
- **Secondary paths.** A second path opens once the primary reaches 2 (3 for Necromancy). No
  secondary path may be higher than the primary.
- **Cost.** A new path costs 7 experience; secondary dots cost current rating × 4. Tremere in good
  standing with the Pyramid pay less.
- **Rolls.** Every Thaumaturgy power rolls Willpower. Necromancy and the others roll what each
  power says.
- **Who can learn.** Thaumaturgy is in-clan for the Tremere and Necromancy for the Giovanni and
  their kin. Others need a teacher. A non-Tremere caught using Thaumaturgy may find a Tremere hit
  team at the door. Koldunic Sorcery is for the Tzimisce only. Assamite sorcerers learn
  Awakening of the Steel, a Thaumaturgy path, in-clan.

### Thaumaturgy

## Rituals

Rituals are longer castings: minutes to hours of work, often with ingredients and a blood cost.

- **Learning.** Rituals are learned by study. The first dot of a sorcery comes
  with one level 1 ritual. After that a vampire studies them from a teacher who knows them, or
  from a grimoire or lore text. Study takes a night for level 1 and about a season of nights for
  level 5.
- **Level.** A vampire can only learn rituals up to its sorcery rating.
- **Casting.** Use the **Rituals** button on the vampire's gizmo bar. Rituals of level 3 and up
  need a **ritual circle** (Misc tab), or an **ossuary altar** for Necromancy. The roll is Intelligence + Occult against the ritual's level + 3.
