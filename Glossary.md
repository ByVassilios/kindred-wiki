# Glossary

The words Kindred use, as the mod uses them.

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Kindred | Vampires, in their own word |
| Kine | Mortals |
| The Embrace | Making a new vampire: draining a mortal to death and giving back a little of the sire's blood |
| Sire, childe | The vampire who Embraced another, and the one Embraced |
| Vitae | Vampire blood, and the blood in a vampire's pool |
| Blood pool | The vitae a vampire holds. Generation sets its size |
| Generation | Steps removed from Caine, the first vampire. Lower is stronger |
| Thin-blood | A vampire of the 14th generation or higher, close to mortal |
| The Kiss | A vampire's bite while feeding, which mortals find pleasant |
| Vessel | Someone a vampire feeds on |
| The Beast | The predator in every vampire, which frenzy lets loose |
| Frenzy | The Beast taking over, from hunger or anger |
| Rötschreck | The red fear: panic at fire or sunlight |
| Torpor | A deathlike sleep, from starvation, grave wounds or by choice |
| Final Death | Being destroyed for good |
| Humanity | The human conscience a vampire keeps. See [Humanity and the Paths](Paths-of-Enlightenment.md) |
| Path of Enlightenment | A morality that isn't human, in place of Humanity |
| Golconda | A rare peace with the Beast |
| Wight | A vampire whose Humanity is gone. Only the Beast is left |
| Discipline | A power of the blood, rated in dots |
| Blood sorcery | Thaumaturgy, Necromancy and Koldunic Sorcery, learned as paths and rituals |
| Ghoul | A mortal or animal fed vampire blood, stronger and ageless while the blood lasts |
| Domitor | The vampire who keeps a ghoul |
| Revenant | A ghoul by birth, from a family bred to it |
| Blood bond | The tie formed by drinking a vampire's blood on three separate nights. Regnant is the vampire, thrall the drinker |
| Diablerie | Drinking another vampire's soul. Can lower the drinker's generation |
| Clan, bloodline | A vampire's lineage, with its Disciplines and weakness. Bloodlines are smaller lines |
| Caitiff | A vampire with no clan |
| Antitribu | A clan's members who went over to the Sabbat |
| Sect | A faction of Kindred: the Camarilla, the Sabbat, the Anarchs, or the Independents |
| Court | A sect's gathering in a settlement, under a Prince or its equal |
| Prince | The ruler of a Camarilla court or domain |
| Elysium | A court's neutral ground, where Kindred meet in peace |
| Domain | A territory a Prince rules. Your colony can become one |
| Boon | A favour owed between Kindred: trivial, minor, major or life |
| Blood hunt | A court's order that a vampire be destroyed by anyone |
| Archon | An enforcer of the Camarilla |
| The Masquerade | Hiding what vampires are from the mortal world |
| Breach | Something outsiders saw that they shouldn't have |
| Coterie | A group of Kindred who stand together. Your colony's vampires |
| Vaulderie | The Sabbat rite that binds a pack with mixed blood |
