# Menus and buttons

Where everything is on screen, and what each tab, button and menu option does. Almost every line
in the mod's windows has a tooltip; hover over it for the details.

## The Kindred tab

Select a vampire and open the **Kindred** tab next to Health and Needs. The heading gives the
vampire's clan, age and generation, its sire or sect, and its experience. Unknown outsiders show
as plain "Kindred" until you learn more about them.

Along the bottom:

| Button | What it opens |
|---|---|
| Lineage | The family tree: sire, childer and everyone above them back to Caine. See [The lineage window](#the-lineage-window) |
| Recent rolls | The roll log. See [The roll log](#the-roll-log) |
| Choose clan and Disciplines | The making menu, for a starting vampire you haven't set up yet. See [Getting started](Getting-Started.md#the-making-menu) |

The tab has five pages.

### Character

| Section | What's there |
|---|---|
| Blood | Vitae left and the most the pool holds. Generation sets the size |
| Experience | Points to spend with the **+** buttons on any page, and about how much it earns a night lately. A new vampire also shows its free starting dots. See [Experience](Experience.md) |
| Ambition | The vampire's current goal and how far along it is, with a **Drop** button. See [ambitions](Experience.md#ambitions) |
| Morality | Humanity or the vampire's Path, with its rating and the Virtues: Conscience or Conviction, Self-Control or Instinct, and Courage. Willpower sits under them, with the points left to spend. Each has a **+** button that spends experience |
| Habits | What the vampire does on its own. See below |
| Merits and Flaws | Picked or rolled at the Embrace |
| Derangements | Lasting madness. See [Humanity and frenzy](Humanity-and-Frenzy.md#derangements) |
| Feeding limits | A Ventrue's one kind of prey, or the kind a Prey Exclusion flaw won't touch. A Ravnos's vice |
| The Masquerade | The colony's breach score, and how much of it this vampire caused. Too much and the Sheriff opens an inquiry |
| Active effects | Buffs and switched-on powers, kept off the health tab (setting "Effects on the Kindred tab") |

**Habits** are four choices per vampire.

| Habit | Choices |
|---|---|
| Blood healing | **Never**. **When safe**: waits until nobody has hit the vampire or been hit by it for a few seconds, and stops before it gets hungry. **Always**: heals in the middle of a fight, keeping a couple of points back |
| Willpower | **Never spend**. **To resist frenzy**, the default: saved for holding the Beast back. **Spend freely**: also buys a sure success on important rolls |
| Feeding | **Vials and vessels**: blood vials and prisoners kept as vessels only. **Also animals and the willing**: adds the colony's animals and colonists who crave the Kiss. **Anyone** |
| Powers | **Only when ordered**. **In a fight**: Disciplines against enemies nearby, but only when the vampire is set to attack or has just been hurt. **Whenever useful**: also outside fights |

### Attributes

The nine Attributes (Strength, Dexterity, Stamina, Charisma, Manipulation, Appearance,
Perception, Intelligence, Wits). They come from the pawn's skills, health and traits, and
experience raises them a dot at a time on top of that. Each tooltip lists the vampire's own powers
that roll it. See [Character sheet](Character-Sheet.md#attributes).

### Disciplines

Every Discipline the vampire has, and the ones it could learn, with its dots and a **+** button.
Each Discipline shows its powers as icons:

- Hover a power to see what it does and which dot unlocks it.
- Click a learned power to take it off the gizmo bar, and click again to put it back. Hidden
  powers still work and still autocast.
- A Discipline at its limit says why: the generation's dot cap, or (for a sorcery path) the
  dots another path needs first.

Out-of-clan Disciplines cost more, and most need three [lessons](Experience.md#lessons) before
the first dot. The **Blood powers** row holds the powers every vampire has (blood buff, Steel the Will,
Look Alive).

### Occult

The Occult rating and its **+** button, then every ritual the vampire knows. Click a ritual to see
its sorcery, level, difficulty, casting time, blood cost, ingredients, and whether it needs a
circle and a target. **Study a ritual** starts learning one from a teacher in the colony; the
progress shows here, in hours, and the work happens at a ritual circle or ossuary altar under
Research.

### Actions

Things the vampire can be told to do.

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| Take lessons | Picks a Discipline and a teacher among the colony's vampires. See [lessons](Experience.md#lessons) |
| Write a treatise | At three dots or more, writes a book other vampires learn from. See [treatises](Experience.md#treatises) |
| Enter torpor | Sinks into torpor on purpose. No blood is used while it sleeps, and wounds heal when it wakes. How long depends on Humanity |

Lessons and treatises are Research work at a table, and their progress shows here. If the vampire
isn't assigned to Research, a red line says so and nothing moves.

## The Mortal tab

On every living mortal. It shows what the person is to the Kindred:

| Line | What it means |
|---|---|
| Regard for the Kindred | A bar from hateful to devoted. See [Feeding](Feeding.md#how-mortals-feel-about-it) |
| Blood bonds | One dot for each night they drank from a vampire. The third is a full bond. Click to select the regnant |
| Thrall | They're under the hold of a vampire from outside the colony |
| The Kiss | They've felt the Kiss and want it again, and how badly |
| Faith | True Faith, as dots |

## The Ghoul tab

On every ghoul, in place of the Mortal tab.

| Line | What it means |
|---|---|
| Ghoul of… | The domitor, and its clan. A revenant names its family instead |
| Vitae | Borrowed blood. A point of it closes a wound, and a drink from the domitor tops it up |
| Next drink needed | Days until withdrawal starts, or a warning that it already has. Revenants make their own vitae |
| A ghoul for… | Years of service. The blood holds back ageing |
| Experience and Disciplines | A ghoul learns only what its domitor's blood carries. Blood of a lower generation lets it go higher |
| The clan's curse | A share of the domitor's clan weakness, from drinking too much of that blood |
| Blood bonds | As on the Mortal tab |

## The gizmo bar

A vampire's buttons, besides vanilla's.

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| Discipline tiles | One tile per Discipline, its powers as small icons in two rows. Click an icon to cast. A tick marks a power that is switched on, and a green bar fills over an icon while it cools down (hover for how long). Turn the setting "Discipline tiles" off for vanilla's one-button-per-power layout |
| A relic's tile | Powers lent by a relic get a tile of their own, named for the relic |
| Rituals | Work a known ritual. The vampire fetches the ingredients first, and from level 3 it has to stand in a ritual circle (or an ossuary altar for Necromancy) |
| Undergo the Suspire | The last test on the road to Golconda. Only at night, and only once. See [Humanity and frenzy](Humanity-and-Frenzy.md#golconda) |
| Swindle a trader | Ravnos only. Far better prices for two hours, with a chance of being caught. Every three days at most |
| Resume own shape | Ends a shapeshift |
| Return to the body | For a vampire riding someone else's body, and on the sleeping body it left |
| Astral journey | Psychic Projection's mind, looking in on far places |
| Rise, Drift | On a vampire sunk into the earth (Earth Meld and its cousins) |
| Step through | Incorporeal Passage, through a wall |
| Restore… | Brings back a limb shrivelled by Thaumaturgy, for blood |
| Draw from the hilt | The Sword of Dracula's stored blood |
| Dispel | On an illusion, to make it go |
| Feed false news | On a colonist caught reporting to a court |

**Power menus.** Some powers ask what to do once they've been paid for: which Attribute the blood
buff raises, which order Dominate gives, what Fleshcraft makes. The menu stays open until you pick
something. When the vampire casts by itself, it picks at random among the options that don't need
you to click on the map.

**Power timers.** With the setting on, the bottom-left inspect pane lists the vampire's running
powers and the time each has left.

## The right-click menu

Select a vampire and right-click someone or something. The mod's orders sit under one **Kindred**
entry (turn the setting "Kindred right-click submenu" off to list them with vanilla's).

| Option | On | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Feed on… | A mortal, animal or vampire who holds still | A careful feeding. On Kindred, each night's drink is a step of the blood bond |
| Drain… dry | A mortal | Takes every drop. The vessel dies, it counts as a sin, and outsiders who find the body know what killed it |
| Give a blood vial to… | Another vampire | Carries a vial over to them |
| Drink blood vial | A vial | Drinks it. A Ventrue refuses the wrong prey |
| Eat… for show | A meal | Eats for the Masquerade, for a Willpower point unless a Merit makes it free. It comes back up later |
| Embrace… | A mortal, alive or freshly dead | Drains them to death to rise as a childe in a few hours. A Camarilla court wants its Prince asked first |
| Ask… for the Embrace | A Kindred prisoner, by a mortal colonist | Offers freedom for the Embrace. Shows the chance they agree |
| Make… a ghoul, Give… a drink of vitae | A mortal or animal, or another vampire | A point of the vampire's blood. Ghouls need a drink every fifteen days or so |
| Drive a stake through…'s heart | A downed vampire | Needs a wooden stake in reach. Quick on a vampire in torpor |
| Stake… with a Nail of Dismus | A helpless vampire | The relic's stake. Drains its Willpower every day |
| Commit diablerie on… | A helpless vampire | Drinks the soul. Can lower the diablerist's generation, at a heavy cost |
| Face the flame of… | A lit torch or campfire | Sits by it and tests its Courage for experience. See [Experience](Experience.md#facing-the-flame) |
| Talk… round | A runaway childe on an investigation site | Charisma + Social to bring it home quietly |
| Send… home as a string | A ghoul, thrall or Dominated outsider | Lets them go home to work for the coterie. See [Sects and courts](Sects-and-Courts.md#influence-over-mortals) |

An animal under **Subsume the Spirit** takes two right-click orders of its own: go somewhere, and
attack someone.

## The Kindred main tab

The **Kindred** button on the bottom bar. Six pages.

| Page | What's on it |
|---|---|
| Overview | The Masquerade score. Your sect and your standing with each sect (-100 to 100), permissions to Embrace, anyone blood hunted. Buttons to declare for a sect, leave it or defect. Once you're a domain, its offices and the gathering you can host. A Sabbat colony's pack offices and the Vaulderie |
| Courts | Every court you know of and its opinion of you. Click one to find it on the world map; **Write to the court** opens its window by letter |
| Boons | Favours owed to your coterie (with **Call it in**) and favours you owe |
| Strings | Mortals in other settlements who work for you, and who holds each. Each can be pulled once a week |
| Chronicle | News from the Kindred world: coups, cities changing hands, blood hunts |
| Our story | Your colony's own history: Embraces, torpor, diablerie, offices won |

## The court window

Opened by a caravan with a vampire at a court's settlement, or by letter from the Courts page.
By letter you can take missions, deal in boons and cast rituals for the court; everything else
needs your Kindred there in person.

| Section | What you can do |
|---|---|
| Standing | The court's opinion of you, and your sect standing. Hover for exactly what raises and lowers it |
| Who holds court | The Prince and the other seats, highest first |
| Your Kindred here | **Present yourself** (each vampire, before missions). Rank and missions done. **Claim the clan's seat** when your opinion is high enough |
| Missions | Offers with their reward and standing. **Take** one; it becomes a quest |
| Elysium | **Attend Elysium** on the nights it's held. Each acknowledged guest raises the opinion |
| What the court can do for you | Services priced in boons. **Pay** with a boon owed to you, or buy **on credit** |
| Owed to this court | **Settle with a boon** you hold that's as big or bigger |
| Other | **Listen for rumours** (once a night, may reveal another court). **Offer a favour** (once a season: a gift, a ritual, a Discipline taught). **Sell a secret** (Nosferatu). **Give your art** (Toreador) |

## The lineage window

A tree with one box per generation, from Caine down to the selected vampire's childer. Each box
names the vampire, its clan and generation, and whether it walks the night, lies in torpor, was
destroyed or was devoured. Unknown ancestors are grey. Click a vampire who is on the map to
jump to it. See [Embrace, bonds and ghouls](Embrace-Bonds-and-Ghouls.md#lineage).

## The roll log

The last 50 rolls the colony's Kindred made: who, what for, the dice and difficulty, and how it
came out. Runs of the same roll are folded into one line ("3 of 5 passed"). Click a line to jump
to the vampire. Colony rolls and big events also show in vanilla's Log tab.

## Letters with choices

Some events ask you to decide.

| Letter | Choices |
|---|---|
| Asks for the blood | Grant it, make them a ghoul instead, promise it for later, or refuse. A promise not kept within a year turns them against you |
| A sire's demand | Pay what the sire asks, or refuse. Refusing too often brings the sire in person |
| The Pyramid's tithe | Tremere only: pay silver or blood, or refuse |
| Inquiry | The Sheriff wants a vampire who caused too much breach: hand them over, lie for them, owe a boon, or refuse |
| A follower of the Path | Take the teaching, or send the mentor away |
| Dissent in the domain | Give the Anarchs a say, or put it down |
| The sleeper | Talk to the old thing from the casket, let it go, or fight |
| A debt called in | Do what the creditor asks before the deadline, or refuse and take the scandal |

## Alerts

| Alert | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Frenzy risk** | These Kindred are hungry or have no Willpower left, and the Beast is close to the surface. A provocation is more likely to set off a frenzy.  Feed them, and let their mood recover to win back Willpower. |
| **Ghoul needs vitae** | These ghouls are close to withdrawal, or already in it.  Without another drink they suffer, and a week into withdrawal the blood's gifts are gone and the years it held back catch up with them. A domitor with blood to spare gives a drink on its own once the night comes. If it can't, select a vampire and right-click the ghoul to give a drink of vitae. |
| **Kindred in torpor** | These Kindred lie in the deathlike sleep of torpor.  Most wake on their own after some days, sooner with high Humanity. One that starved into torpor stays down until it holds enough blood: carry it blood vials or give it vitae. Keep them under a roof and away from enemies. |
| **Kindred outside near dawn** | The sun will be up within two hours, and these vampires are under open sky. Get them under a roof before it rises, or they will burn. |
| **Kindred starving** | These Kindred are nearly out of vitae. When the next night's blood comes due they fall into torpor if they can't pay it, and before then the sight of a vessel may hand them over to the Beast in a hunger frenzy.  Feed them, or give them blood vials. |
| **Masquerade: …** | Too many people have seen what your Kindred are, and the Masquerade is cracking. Traders stay away at night, hunters take an interest, and it gets worse from here.  Keep feeding and powers out of sight of outsiders, and deal with the witnesses who got away. |
| **Outsiders present** | Mortals from outside the colony are here, and they don't know what your Kindred are.  Anything they see could break the Masquerade: feeding, obvious powers, frenzy, a wound that closes too fast. Keep it out of their sight until they leave. |
| **Witnesses on the map** | These outsiders saw something they shouldn't have. If they leave the map alive and still remember it, the breach of the Masquerade goes up.  Kill them, take them prisoner, make them forget it with Dominate, bind their tongue with a ritual, or recruit them into the colony. |
