# Menus and buttons

Where everything is on screen, and what each tab, button and menu option does. Almost every line
in the mod's windows has a tooltip; hover over it for the details.

## The Kindred tab

Select a vampire and open the **Kindred** tab next to Health and Needs. The heading gives the
vampire's clan, age and generation, its sire or sect, its blood and its experience. Unknown
outsiders show as plain "Kindred" until you learn more about them. Once the Masquerade is no
longer intact, a red line under the blood gives the breach score on every page.

The pages are down the left side. A gold number beside a page counts what you can do there now: free
Discipline dots to spend, or Ambitions waiting to be picked. Click a heading's name to fold
the section under it; the page remembers that until you quit the game.

Along the bottom:

| Button | What it opens |
|---|---|
| Lineage | The family tree: sire, childer and everyone above them back to Caine. See [The lineage window](#the-lineage-window) |
| Recent rolls | The roll log. See [The roll log](#the-roll-log) |
| Combination powers | On the Disciplines page: every combination power and what it needs |
| Choose clan and Disciplines | The making menu, for a starting vampire you haven't set up yet. See [Getting started](Getting-Started.md#the-making-menu) |

Click a Discipline, morality, merit or derangement to read its page in the
[grimoire](#the-grimoire), and the **?** at the end of a heading opens the guide on that subject.

| Page | What's on it |
|---|---|
| Sheet | Clan rank, morality, Virtues, Willpower, Merits and Flaws, derangements, active effects |
| Attributes | The nine Attributes |
| Disciplines | Blood powers, Disciplines, combination powers, paths not open yet |
| Occult | The Occult rating, rituals and lore books |
| The Wolf | An Abomination's wolf half: Rage, Gnosis, Gifts and rites |
| Growth | The Ambition, lessons and treatises. Your own vampires only |
| Court | The seat it holds at a court, and its familiar |
| Backgrounds | Allies, Contacts, Herd, Resources and the rest. See [The sworn court](The-Sworn-Court.md) |
| Conduct | Habits, how it fights, how it covers up, and Enter torpor. Your own vampires only |

### Sheet

| Section | What's there |
|---|---|
| Clan rank | Its rank in the clan's own politics |
| Morality | Humanity or the vampire's Path, with its rating and the Virtues: Conscience or Conviction, Self-Control or Instinct, and Courage. Willpower sits under them. Hover the next dot for its cost and click it to buy |
| Abomination | Only on an Abomination: what the wolf half costs it |
| Merits and Flaws | Picked or rolled at the Embrace |
| Feeding limits | A Ventrue's one kind of prey, or the kind a Prey Exclusion flaw won't touch. A Ravnos's vice |
| Derangements | Lasting madness. See [Humanity and frenzy](Humanity-and-Frenzy.md#derangements) |
| Active effects | Buffs and switched-on powers, kept off the health tab (setting "Effects on the Kindred tab") |

### Attributes

The nine Attributes in three columns, Physical, Social and Mental, as on the printed sheet. They
come from the pawn's skills, health and traits, and experience raises them a dot at a time on top of
that: hover the next dot for its cost and click it to buy. Each tooltip lists the vampire's own
powers that roll it. See [Character sheet](Character-Sheet.md#attributes).

### Disciplines

Every Discipline the vampire has, and the ones it could learn, with its dots.
A new vampire's free starting dots show in gold under the heading.
Each Discipline shows its powers as icons:

- Hover a power to see what it does and which dot unlocks it.
- Click a learned power to take it off the gizmo bar, and click again to put it back. Hidden
  powers still work and still autocast.
- A Discipline at its limit says why: the generation's dot cap, or (for a sorcery path) the
  dots another path needs first.

Out-of-clan Disciplines cost more, and most need three [lessons](Experience.md#lessons) before
the first dot. The **Blood powers** row holds the powers every vampire has (blood buff, Steel the Will,
Look Alive).

### Occult

The Occult rating, then every ritual the vampire knows. Click a ritual to see
its sorcery, level, difficulty, casting time, blood cost, ingredients, and whether it needs a
circle and a target. **Study a ritual** starts learning one from a teacher in the colony; the
progress shows here, in hours, and the work happens at a ritual circle or ossuary altar under
Research.

**Lore books** lists the lore books on the colony's maps: those the vampire would still learn from
first (started ones with a bar), then the ones it has read, then the ones it can't learn from yet,
greyed with the reason. Hover a book for what it teaches; click it to find it.

### Growth

The vampire's **Ambition** and how far along it is, with a **Drop** button. See
[ambitions](Experience.md#ambitions).

Under **Study**:

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| Take lessons | Picks a Discipline and a teacher among the colony's vampires. See [lessons](Experience.md#lessons) |
| Write a treatise | At three dots or more, writes a book other vampires learn from. See [treatises](Experience.md#treatises) |

Lessons and treatises are Research work at a table, and their progress shows here. If the vampire
isn't assigned to Research, a red line says so and nothing moves.

### Court

The seat the vampire holds at the court the colony is sworn to, and its familiar, if it has one:
how far away the animal is and which Discipline it has lent.

### Conduct

**Habits** are four choices per vampire.

| Habit | Choices |
|---|---|
| Blood healing | **Never**. **When safe**: waits until nobody has hit the vampire or been hit by it for a few seconds, and stops before it gets hungry. **Always**: heals in the middle of a fight, keeping a couple of points back |
| Willpower | **Never spend**. **To resist frenzy**, the default: saved for holding the Beast back. **Spend freely**: also buys a sure success on important rolls |
| Feeding | **Vials and vessels**: blood vials and prisoners kept as vessels only. **Also animals and the willing**: adds the colony's animals, colonists who crave the Kiss and colonists devoted to the Kindred. **Anyone** |
| Powers | **Only when ordered**. **In a fight**: Disciplines against enemies nearby, but only when the vampire is set to attack or has just been hurt. **Whenever useful**: also outside fights |

**In a fight** picks the vampire's fighting style. **The Masquerade** has the cover-up switch, each
witness on the map with the surest way to deal with them, and the colony's breach score with how
much of it this vampire caused. Too much and the Sheriff opens an inquiry.

Under **Orders**, **Enter torpor** sinks the vampire into torpor on purpose. No blood is used while
it sleeps, and wounds heal when it wakes. How long depends on Humanity.

## The Mortal tab

On every living mortal. It shows what the person is to the Kindred:

| Line | What it means |
|---|---|
| Regard for the Kindred | A bar from hateful to devoted. See [Feeding](Feeding.md#how-mortals-feel-about-it) |
| Blood bonds | One dot for each night they drank from a vampire. The third is a full bond. Click to select the regnant |
| Thrall | They're under the hold of a vampire from outside the colony |
| The Kiss | They've felt the Kiss and want it again, and how badly |
| Faith | True Faith, as dots |

## The Ghoul tab

On every ghoul, in place of the Mortal tab. Its Disciplines show five dots like a vampire's; the
dots past the ghoul's cap are faint. With the setting for the ghoul's full sheet on, its pages (Sheet, Attributes,
Disciplines and Conduct) are icons down the left side; hover one for its name.

| Line | What it means |
|---|---|
| Ghoul of… | The domitor, and its clan. A revenant names its family instead |
| Vitae | Borrowed blood. A point of it closes a wound, and a drink from the domitor tops it up |
| Next drink needed | Days until withdrawal starts, or a warning that it already has. Revenants make their own vitae |
| A ghoul for… | Years of service. The blood holds back ageing |
| Experience and Disciplines | A ghoul learns only what its domitor's blood carries. Blood of a lower generation lets it go higher |
| The clan's curse | A share of the domitor's clan weakness, from drinking too much of that blood |
| Blood bonds | As on the Mortal tab |

## The gizmo bar

A vampire's buttons, besides vanilla's.

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| Discipline tiles | One tile per Discipline, its powers as small icons in two rows. Click an icon to cast, right-click it to read the power in the grimoire, and click the tile's name for the Discipline's page. A tick marks a power that is switched on, and a green bar fills over an icon while it cools down (hover for how long). Turn the setting "Power tiles" off for vanilla's one-button-per-power layout |
| A relic's tile | Powers lent by a relic get a tile of their own, named for the relic |
| Rituals | Work a known ritual. The vampire fetches the ingredients first, and from level 3 it has to stand in a ritual circle (or an ossuary altar for Necromancy) |
| Undergo the Suspire | The last test on the road to Golconda. Only at night, and only once. See [Humanity and frenzy](Humanity-and-Frenzy.md#golconda) |
| Swindle a trader | Ravnos only. Far better prices for two hours, with a chance of being caught. Every three days at most |
| Resume own shape | Ends a shapeshift |
| Return to the body | For a vampire riding someone else's body, and on the sleeping body it left |
| Astral journey | Psychic Projection's mind, looking in on far places |
| Rise, Drift | On a vampire sunk into the earth (Earth Meld and its cousins) |
| Step through | Incorporeal Passage, through a wall |
| Restore… | Brings back a limb shrivelled by Thaumaturgy, for blood |
| Draw from the hilt | The Sword of Dracula's stored blood |
| Dispel | On an illusion, to make it go |
| Feed false news | On a colonist caught reporting to a court |
| Unleash | On a drafted colonist, beside Fire at will. It goes after the nearest enemy anywhere on the map and fights as an enemy of its kind would: powers first, closing in unseen, pulling back to heal when badly hurt. Anyone with a gun backs away from melee fighters and looks for cover. A pawn scared by a power stays on the map and goes back to the fight when the panic ends. Your orders come first, and once one is done it goes back to the hunt. Undrafting ends it. While it's on, a **Pick target** button chooses the enemy it goes after. Once that enemy is down or gone it picks the nearest one again, and clicking the button clears the target early. A power aimed out of range or out of sight walks the pawn closer, then casts. |

**Power menus.** Some powers ask what to do once they've been paid for: which Attribute the blood
buff raises, which order Dominate gives, what Fleshcraft makes. The menu stays open until you pick
something. When the vampire casts by itself, it picks at random among the options that don't need
you to click on the map.

**Unleashed by day.** An unleashed vampire caught by daylight stays where it is and only uses its
powers.

**Drafted orders.** A job you give a drafted vampire (feeding, a ritual, a power with a walk to
it) carries on after you undraft it.

**Power timers.** With the setting on, the bottom-left inspect pane lists the vampire's running
powers and the time each has left.

## The right-click menu

Select a vampire and right-click someone or something. The mod's orders sit under one **Kindred**
entry (turn the setting "Kindred right-click submenu" off to list them with vanilla's).

| Option | On | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Feed on… | A mortal, animal or vampire who holds still | A careful feeding. On Kindred, each night's drink is a step of the blood bond |
| Drain… dry | A mortal | Takes every drop. The vessel dies, it counts as a sin, and outsiders who find the body know what killed it |
| Give a blood vial to… | Another vampire | Carries a vial over to them |
| Drink blood vial | A vial | Drinks it. A Ventrue refuses the wrong prey |
| Eat… for show | A meal | Eats for the Masquerade, for a Willpower point unless a Merit makes it free. It comes back up later |
| Embrace… | A mortal, alive or freshly dead | Drains them to death to rise as a childe in a few hours. A Camarilla court wants its Prince asked first |
| Ask… for the Embrace | A Kindred prisoner, by a mortal colonist | Offers freedom for the Embrace. Shows the chance they agree |
| Make… a ghoul, Give… a drink of vitae | A mortal or animal, or another vampire | A point of the vampire's blood. Ghouls need a drink every fifteen days or so |
| Drive a stake through…'s heart | A downed vampire | Needs a wooden stake in reach. Quick on a vampire in torpor |
| Stake… with a Nail of Dismus | A helpless vampire | The relic's stake. Drains its Willpower every day |
| Commit diablerie on… | A helpless vampire | Drinks the soul. Can lower the diablerist's generation, at a heavy cost |
| Face the flame of… | A lit torch or campfire | Sits by it and tests its Courage for experience. See [Experience](Experience.md#facing-the-flame) |
| Talk… round | A runaway childe on an investigation site | Charisma + Social to bring it home quietly |
| Send… home as a string | A ghoul, thrall or Dominated outsider | Lets them go home to work for the coterie. See [Sects and courts](Sects-and-Courts.md#influence-over-mortals) |

An animal under **Subsume the Spirit** takes two right-click orders of its own: go somewhere, and
attack someone.

## The Kindred main tab

The **Kindred** button on the bottom bar. Six pages. While the colony has two or more kinds from the Rim by Night mods, only the window used last keeps its button, and a list down the left side of each window names every one with its pages, so a click opens any page of any of them; the Core setting **One bottom-bar button** turns that off. Anything you open from one of these pages (a court, Ask on the Boons page, Call it in, the Red List, an office pick) opens inside the same window, with a path at the top such as "Boons › Teacher: Dominate › Who it is for": each step in it is a link back, **< Back** goes up one step and the page's name in the left list goes back to the page. The window widens while something wider than the page is open.

| Page | What's on it |
|---|---|
| Overview | The Masquerade score. Your sect and your standing with each sect (-100 to 100), permissions to Embrace, anyone blood hunted. Buttons to declare for a sect, leave it or defect. Once you're a domain, its offices and the gathering you can host. A Sabbat colony's pack offices and the Vaulderie. **Read the Red List** opens the [Red List](Sects-and-Courts.md#the-red-list) window |
| Courts | Every court you know of: court or clan haven, the settlement it meets in, its Prince and its opinion of you. Click one to find it on the world map; **Open the court** opens its window: in person while your Kindred are there, else by letter |
| Boons: ledger | One list of the favours owed to your coterie and the favours you owe, owed to you first, with the totals each way on top. Each row says who, their seat, where they are now and why the boon is owed. **Call it in** lets you pick a favour from the debtor, up to the boon's size. Anything it can't do is greyed, with the reason |
| Boons: ask the courts | What the courts you know can do for you now. A list down the left picks the price (trivial, minor, major, life). Its services sit under headers that fold open and shut: Secrets, Teaching, Blood and the Embrace, The Masquerade, Force and The Prince's favour. Each row names the vampire who would do it: a Tremere teaches the rituals it knows, an ancilla or elder teaches a Discipline it has, the Prince grants permits and pardons, the Sheriff deals with hunters. A row says when it comes free from your own Prince or when a boon you hold pays for it. **Ask...** opens a window with a row for each vampire who can do it and a button for each way to pay: **Free** from your own Prince, **Settle** a boon that vampire owes you, **Pay with a boon...** someone else owes you if it is the price or bigger (no change comes back), or **On credit**. A way you can't use is greyed; hover it for the reason. Hover a row for what it does |
| Strings | Mortals in other settlements who work for you, and who holds each. Each can be pulled once a week |

The world's news and the colony's own story are on the Chronicle and Our story pages under Rim by Night, at the top of the list down the window's left side. Each page holds the lines of every mod you have loaded; click a mod on its left for only that mod's lines, or a name under it for one colonist or home. Kindred write Embraces, torpor, diablerie and offices won there, and the courts' news tells of plots and showdowns, cities changing hands and blood hunts.

## The court window

Opened from the Courts page. With a vampire of yours at the court, walking its town or in a
caravan on its tile, it is in person. Otherwise it is a letter: you can take missions, buy
services and cast rituals for the court, and everything else needs your Kindred there.

It has three pages, four at a Camarilla or Anarch court, which always has more than one coterie.
The boons you are owed and owe are on the Kindred tab.

| Page | Section | What you can do |
|---|---|---|
| Court | Standing | The court's opinion of you, and your sect standing. Hover for exactly what raises and lowers it |
| Court | Who holds court | The Prince and the other seats, highest first, each with its coterie |
| Coteries | The cards | A card for each coterie: temperament, how it stands with the Prince's, members, power in ten cells, and "Plotting" on the plotters. Click one to pick it |
| Coteries | The picked coterie | What it wants and pays, its members (leader marked), its missions with **Take**, your record with it, and during a plot the odds and **Back the Prince**, **Back the plotters**, **Stay out** |
| Court | Your Kindred here | **Present yourself** (each vampire, before missions; walking into the town does it for you). Rank and missions done. **Claim the clan's seat** when your opinion is high enough |
| Court | Elysium | **Attend Elysium** on the nights it's held. Each acknowledged guest raises the opinion |
| Missions | Offers | Offers with their reward and standing, and which coterie offers each. **Take** one; it becomes a quest |
| Missions | Under way | Your missions for this court or ending here. A caravan there hands over a vessel, a sealed casket or a runaway childe, stands guard at Elysium, or does a coterie's errand |

The buttons along the bottom: **Listen for rumours** (once a night: Golconda, another court, or where an old one sleeps). **Offer a favour** (once a season: a gift, a ritual, a Discipline taught). **Sell a secret** (Nosferatu). **Give your art** (Toreador).

## The lineage window

A tree with one box per generation, from Caine down to the selected vampire's childer. Each box
names the vampire, its clan and generation, and whether it walks the night, lies in torpor, was
destroyed or was devoured. Unknown ancestors are grey. Click a vampire who is on the map to
jump to it. See [Embrace, bonds and ghouls](Embrace-Bonds-and-Ghouls.md#lineage).

## The roll log

The last 50 rolls the colony's Kindred made: who, what for, the dice and difficulty, and how it
came out. Runs of the same roll are folded into one line ("3 of 5 passed"). Under each line a
grey line says what the pool was made of and what set the difficulty ("Manipulation 3 + Social 2 +
aura read 2, difficulty 7 (Mara's Willpower)"). Click a line to jump to the vampire. Colony rolls
and big events also show in vanilla's Log tab.

**Dice pools.** Hover a power on a vampire's Discipline tile to see the roll it makes: each part with
its dice, the total and the difficulty. Before the power is aimed, a difficulty set by the target
reads as a rule ("against the target's Willpower"). While a mental power is aimed, the odds under
the cursor list the same parts. A power's grimoire page has the formula in a box at the top
("Manipulation + Social", then the difficulty).

## Letters with choices

Some events ask you to decide.

| Letter | Choices |
|---|---|
| Asks for the blood | Grant it, make them a ghoul instead, promise it for later, or refuse (right-clicking the letter away refuses). A promise not kept within a year turns them against you. Each pawn has one letter open at a time, and a dying wish comes at most once a day |
| A sire's demand | Pay what the sire asks, or refuse. Refusing too often brings the sire in person |
| The Pyramid's tithe | Tremere only: pay silver or blood, or refuse |
| Inquiry | The Sheriff wants a vampire who caused too much breach: hand them over, lie for them, owe a boon, or refuse |
| Dissent in the domain | Give the Anarchs a say, or put it down |
| The sleeper | Talk to the old thing from the casket, let it go, or fight |
| A debt called in | Do what the creditor asks before the deadline, or refuse and take the scandal |

Some offers go to the Quests tab instead, where you accept them or let them run out: someone at the
door (two days), a follower of a Path offering to teach (five days), an empty domain to claim (ten
days) and survivors of a fallen home asking to join (two days).

## Choice lists

Most menus open a list beside the window you clicked in, or above the gizmo. A long list has filters down the left with a count beside each, and **Can pick only** hides what can't be had yet. Click a row to read about it on the right, then press **Choose**, double-click it, or press Enter. A greyed row says under it what's missing. A list with nothing to read takes the row on one click.

## The grimoire

The book button in the bottom-right corner, beside vanilla's play settings, opens the grimoire: a
panel on the right edge that leaves the game running. It covers the alerts and letters while it's
open, and its X closes it. It holds every clan, Discipline, power, ritual, merit, item and creature the mod adds, made from the game's own data, with this guide in the Kindred chapter. The setting "Grimoire", under The Rim by Night: Core in the mod settings, hides it.

- **Selected** shows what to do about whoever or whatever you have selected, and links to read
  more. It changes as you select something else. A vampire or Garou hiding among your prisoners
  shows nothing of what it is.
- **Ask** looks through every chapter. A question works ("how do I heal"): each answer shows the sentence it
  found, and clicking one opens that part of the page.
- **Browse** lists every chapter, with a "what to do" section at the top of each mod's.
- Gold, underlined words open their own page. **Back** returns to the page before.
- The info button beside a page's title opens the game's own card, with every stat.
- From the game: click a row on the Kindred tab, right-click a power's icon, or press the **?**
  beside a tab heading.
- The book opens where you left it until you restart the game.

## Alerts

| Alert | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Frenzy risk** | These Kindred are hungry or have no Willpower left, and the Beast is close to the surface. A provocation is more likely to set off a frenzy.  Feed them, and let their mood recover to win back Willpower. |
| **Ghoul needs vitae** | These ghouls are close to withdrawal, or already in it.  Without another drink they suffer, and a week into withdrawal the blood's gifts are gone and the years it held back catch up with them. A domitor with blood to spare gives a drink on its own once the night comes. If it can't, select a vampire and right-click the ghoul to give a drink of vitae. |
| **Kindred in torpor** | These Kindred lie in the deathlike sleep of torpor.  Most wake on their own after some days, sooner with high Humanity. One that starved into torpor stays down until it holds enough blood: carry it blood vials or give it vitae. Keep them under a roof and away from enemies. |
| **Kindred outside near dawn** | The sun will be up within two hours, and these vampires are under open sky. Get them under a roof before it rises, or they will burn. |
| **Kindred starving** | These Kindred are nearly out of vitae. When the next night's blood comes due they fall into torpor if they can't pay it, and before then the sight of a vessel may hand them over to the Beast in a hunger frenzy.  Feed them, or give them blood vials. |
| **Outsiders present** | Mortals from outside the colony are here, and they don't know what your Kindred are.  Anything they see could break the Masquerade: feeding, obvious powers, frenzy, a wound that closes too fast. Keep it out of their sight until they leave. |

The Masquerade alert (**Masquerade: …**) shows from Suspected on, red from Exposed. It is Core's
cover alert now, which also lists the Veil, a cell's exposure or a mage's Disbelief when those
mods are loaded.
