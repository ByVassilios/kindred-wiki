# Getting started

## Install

1. Subscribe to [Harmony](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2009463077) and to
   [The Rim by Night: Kindred](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3824249945) on the Steam Workshop. Without Steam, download Harmony's release and
   put the `Kindred` folder from the mod's GitHub repository in RimWorld's `Mods` folder.
2. In the mod list, load Harmony first, then The Rim by Night: Kindred.

The mod can go into a save that's already running. Nothing changes until someone becomes Kindred
or a vampire shows up from outside.

## Your first vampire

There are three ways to get one.

**Start with one.** Pick one of the mod's scenarios when you make a new colony:

| Scenario | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Lone neonate** | Your sire made you, taught you almost nothing and left you on a transport bound for the rim. You woke in the hold with a crate of stolen blood and nobody to answer to. Now you have a planet to hide on, and the sun comes up in a few hours.<br><br>Note: one fledgling vampire with no one to feed on. A hard start. |
| **Camarilla coterie** | A prince far away wants a foothold on this world and sent three young Kindred to make one. Two mortal retainers came along to keep the lights on by day and the coterie fed by night. Make a domain the Camarilla can be proud of, and keep the Masquerade while you do it. |
| **Sabbat pack** | Three Kindred of the Sword of Caine, sent to take this world from the Camarilla, with two mortals who know better than to ask questions. The pack has already shared the Vaulderie, and the Camarilla counts you as its enemy. |
| **Anarch gang** | Three Kindred who walked out on their prince and kept walking, all the way to a new world, with two mortal friends who came along for the ride. Nobody here tells you what to do. |
| **Independent clan crew** | Two Kindred of one of the Independent clans, sent out by their elders on clan business, with three mortals to do the work by day. The clan already has a haven on this world, and it is expecting you. |
| **The stowaway** | The three of you wake to sirens and tearing metal and barely make the escape pods. One of you was never really asleep. They boarded the ship hidden in a cryptosleep casket, and the others are about to find out what they are. |

You can also add the "starting Kindred" part to any scenario in the vanilla scenario editor. It
sets how many starting colonists are Kindred, their generation range, and whether the others
know.

**Be Embraced.** A vampire who comes to the colony can make a colonist into one of its childer.
See [Embrace, bonds and ghouls](Embrace-Bonds-and-Ghouls.md).

**Dev mode.** Turn on dev mode, open the debug actions menu, and under "Vampires" use **Make
vampire** on a colonist. It rolls a clan and everything else for you. **Open making menu** lets
you pick instead.

## The making menu

Every starting Kindred without a sire in the colony gets a window when the game begins. The game
pauses while it's open.

| Choice | What it decides |
|---|---|
| Clan | Disciplines, weakness and calling. See [Clans](Clans.md) |
| Generation | Blood pool size and how strong the vampire can become. 10th to 13th by default |
| Sect | Defaults to the clan's. The first Kindred's sect becomes the colony's |
| Age | Neonate (1 to 40 years undead) by default. Ancillae (50 to 150) and elders (200 to 300) start with more dots and experience. Every start comes with a sire who wants things |
| Discipline dots | Three dots among the clan's Disciplines |
| Merit and Flaw | One of each, either, or neither. See [Character sheet](Character-Sheet.md) |

"Roll for me" fills in anything you leave blank. Closing the window does the same.

## Where to look

- **The Kindred tab** on any vampire, next to Health and Needs: blood, experience, Humanity,
  Virtues, Habits, Attributes, Disciplines and rituals, over four pages.
- **The Kindred main tab** on the bottom bar: the Masquerade, your sect, the courts, boons and the
  chronicle.
- **The Vitae bar** in the Needs tab. It replaces Food and Rest.
- **The gizmo bar**: a tile per Discipline with its powers, and buttons for rituals and the rest.
- **The Kindred right-click submenu**: feeding, the Embrace, ghouls, stakes and diablerie.
- **Alerts** on the right: dawn coming, starving vampires, frenzy risk, witnesses, the Masquerade.

Every tab, button and menu option is explained on [Menus and buttons](Interface.md).

## Your first night: a checklist

1. **Roof.** Make sure every vampire can reach a roofed spot before dawn. Better still, build a
   coffin (Furniture tab) and assign it like a bed.
2. **Schedule.** Vampires sleep 06:00 to 18:00 on their own. Copy a vampire's schedule in the
   Schedule tab to move mortals who work with them onto the same hours.
3. **Blood.** Plan where blood will come from. Willing colonists, prisoners kept as vessels and
   blood vials from a medical bed all work. Read [Feeding](Feeding.md).
4. **Healing.** Set each vampire's heal policy under Habits on the Kindred tab: never, when safe, or always.
   Only blood heals them; doctors can't.
5. **Fire.** Keep vampires away from fire. Firefighting is off for them by default, and you
   should leave it that way.
6. **Witnesses.** Visitors and traders are outsiders. Don't feed, frenzy or use loud powers in
   front of them. See [The Masquerade and hunters](Masquerade-and-Hunters.md).
