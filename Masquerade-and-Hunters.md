# The Masquerade and hunters

The Masquerade is the one rule every vampire shares: mortals must not learn that Kindred exist.
Your colonists already know. Everyone else is an outsider.

## What counts as a breach

Every power is quiet, subtle or overt. Overt acts include feeding in view, frenzy, burning in the
sun, healing a wound in front of someone, rising from torpor, an undisguised Nosferatu, and a
drained corpse with fang marks. Subtle acts include small powers, shop talk between Kindred, and
being dug out of the ground at dawn.

## Witnesses

- When something overt happens, every outsider who can see it becomes a witness. Subtle acts only
  count if the outsider passes a Perception roll.
- Colonists, ghouls and bonded thralls never count. Prisoners count only if you let them go.
- Hostile raiders who see something overt may panic and run, but they're still witnesses.
- Visitors and traders get nervous and may leave early.

### Silencing a witness

A witness stops counting if, before they leave the map, they:

- die or are captured;
- have the memory taken (Dominate 3);
- are bound or discredited (a full blood bond, some rituals);
- are Embraced or ghouled.

A witness who leaves alive adds to the **breach score**.

## The breach score

One number from 0 to 100, in the Kindred main tab. It fades slowly while nothing new happens.

| Level | Score | What the world does |
|---|---|---|
| Intact | 0-19 | Nothing |
| Rumours | 20-39 | Visitors are warier. Your sect sends a warning |
| Suspected | 40-59 | Hunters start looking. No traders at night. Camarilla standing drops |
| Exposed | 60-79 | Hunter raids by day with fire and stakes. The Camarilla wants it fixed, or calls a blood hunt |
| Shattered | 80-100 | The Society of Leopold: organised daylight assaults with True Faith and fire |

You can turn the whole system off, or scale how fast breaches count and fade, in the mod
settings.

## Hunters

**Wandering hunters** look like any traveller. They pass through, trade, rest, and are friendly
until they see a vampire at work or spot fang marks. Then they turn hostile, or slip away and come
back with friends. They carry stakes and fire, and some have True Faith. They show up more often
once the Masquerade is suspected.

**Hunter raids** come by day from Exposed. **The Society of Leopold** comes when the Masquerade is
shattered.

A dead hunter may leave a notebook naming the breach that brought them.

Hunters finish what they start. A fallen vampire gets a stake, then sun, fire or the head. By day
they go straight for where the Kindred sleep. A heart kept outside its body (Serpentis 5) is
crushed, or half the time pocketed: the hunter runs for the map edge, and if he gets away the
Society keeps the heart at a hideout on the world map for six days, then burns it. Send a caravan
before then. Hunters who escape come back with the next raid. A **Society plant** may also join
the colony, waiting to stake a vampire in its daysleep.

Hunters carry the weapons vampires fear:

| Weapon | Who carries it | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Wooden stake | Hunters (spares), rogue thugs | A weak melee weapon. A heart hit can pin a vampire |
| Stake crossbow | Hunters, Leopold soldiers | Slow to reload. Its bolts pin like a stake |
| Fire lance | Firebrands | Short-range flame that sets the target alight, then a spear |
| Incendiary shotgun | Leopold soldiers | A shotgun whose blasts often start a fire |
| Aspergillum | Leopold Inquisitors | A mace. With True Faith, every blow burns a vampire |

All of them can be crafted. In a world held to a lower tech level (by a mod like Rimedieval or World
Tech Level), raiders only carry weapons from that era or earlier.

## True Faith

Some mortals have True Faith, a holy conviction that burns vampires. A vampire touched by it
recoils, and it can trigger Rötschreck. At Faith 3 or more, a holy symbol carried into melee, or an
aspergillum in hand, burns any vampire it strikes. Your own mortal colonists can gain it through their deeds
against Kindred. Vitae, a blood bond or the Embrace takes it away.

## Enemy Kindred

Hostile vampires cast their combat powers, heal with blood, and stop to
feed on your downed. They come only at night. They wait out of sight at the edge of the map until
someone is alone outdoors, then strike, and they leave before dawn. Badly hurt, they run.

| Threat | What it is |
|---|---|
| Rogue bands | Kindred raiders who carry off the downed, torpid elders included |
| Archons | Camarilla enforcers, sent after a blood hunt or very low standing |
| Sabbat packs | With shovelheads: freshly Embraced, mindless thugs |
| Anarch gangs | Anarch enforcers |
| An elder | An old vampire and its ghouls. If it gets away, it holds a grudge and comes back |
| Assassins | An Assamite sent for one of your Kindred |

### The enemy within

Watch your colonists too. A mortal may be an outside vampire's thrall, a Setite's corrupted
plant, or a Sabbat infiltrator who joined to call a pack. They leak secrets, steal, or tell
hunters where your haven is.

## Someone at the door

Now and then, at night, someone asks to be let in: a vampire fleeing a blood hunt, an abandoned
fledgling, a runaway ghoul, a survivor of a fallen court, or your own sire. The letter may not say everything, and
Auspex can read what they hide.

## The black market

Ghoul dealers sometimes visit a colony with Kindred. They sell vials of Kindred vitae, blood
vials and willing vessels already hooked on the Kiss, and buy blood. Every visit gets talked
about, and hunters sometimes follow.
