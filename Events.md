# Events

What can happen to a colony with Kindred in it, grouped by the setting that switches it off (Mod
settings, World and events). Switching a group off stops new events of that kind; anything already
under way plays out. Most only come to a colony that has Kindred.

## Hunters

Setting: **Hunters**. Driven by the Masquerade breach score, see
[The Masquerade and hunters](Masquerade-and-Hunters.md).

| Event | What happens |
|---|---|
| Travellers | Visitors who are really hunters or investigators. Investigators ask around about what was seen, and once they have a name they pack up to pass it on. Stop them before they leave the map |
| The hunters have a name | An investigator got away. A group of hunters comes within a few days, straight for the vampire they named |
| Hunters in daylight | From the Exposed breach level: an armed band arrives while the sun is up, with fire and stakes |
| The Society of Leopold | At Shattered: the Inquisition's heirs, in force and by day |

## Sect politics

Setting: **Sect politics**. A sect sends its enforcers when your standing with it falls low or it
has called a blood hunt on your Kindred. The Sabbat also comes for a Lasombra antitribu sheltering
with you.

| Event | What happens |
|---|---|
| Archons | The Camarilla's enforcers |
| Sabbat pack | A war pack, with shovelheads: freshly Embraced cannon fodder |
| Anarch gang | The Anarchs, come to settle a score |
| The enemy within | A colonist turns out to be an outside vampire's thrall, slipping off to feed it every few nights |
| Sabbat infiltrator | A newcomer who is a Sabbat court's plant, and calls a pack when the time comes |
| Anarch unrest | A colony vampire stirs up the Anarchs against the domain's Prince. Hear them out or put it down |
| Assamite assassin | Someone paid for one of your Kindred's death. One assassin comes alone and waits for the right moment |
| Setite corruption | A colonist picks up a habit somewhere and won't say where |
| Tremere hit team | Thaumaturgy used outside the clan where Camarilla eyes saw it. The Tremere come to take it back |

## Kindred attacks

Setting: **Kindred attacks**.

| Event | What happens |
|---|---|
| Rogue Kindred | A band of vampires with no court to answer to. They often wait out of sight for someone alone |
| Raid with a vampire | A night raid with one vampire hidden among the raiders, for a colony with no Kindred yet |
| An elder comes | A vampire of great age with a retinue of ghouls. Wounds barely slow it; fire and the sun still work. One that gets away comes back |
| Haunting | Someone killed cruelly here comes back as a ghost and frightens everyone near until it fades or is banished |

## Kindred visitors

Setting: **Kindred visitors**.

| Event | What happens |
|---|---|
| Someone at the door | A Kindred asking for shelter: a fugitive with a blood hunt on them, an abandoned fledgling, a survivor of a fallen court, a hunted sire, or a pale stranger for a colony with no Kindred yet. Auspex can read what they hide |
| A Path's mentor | A follower of a Path offers to teach a vampire who has fallen far enough |
| Occult trader | A caravan with unidentified relics, lore texts, stakes, holy symbols and blood vials. Only colonies with Kindred see it |
| Ghoul dealers | The black market: blood vials, blood wine, vials of Kindred vitae, and vessels hooked on the Kiss |
| Anonymous benefactor | Unsigned gifts arrive by drop pod. Later the sender names a price |
| Revenant joins | A ghoul by birth, from one of the revenant families, asks to join |

## Always on, with their own settings

| Event | Setting | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| A sire's demand | Sire calls | A sire out in the world writes now and then, as its temperament takes it: a demand for vitae, a gift, a letter of teaching, an introduction to a court. A possessive sire refused too often comes in person to take its childe home |
| The wight returns | Wights come back | A vampire lost to the Beast who escaped comes back in the dark, hungrier |
| The world in the background | Background world | Courts change while you play: coups, rival schemes, blood hunts, cities going over to another sect |

## From the courts

These arrive as letters from the courts. See [Sects and courts](Sects-and-Courts.md).

- **A letter from court.** A court learns of your Kindred and tells you where it meets.
- **Invited to Elysium.** A court expects your acknowledged Kindred on a given night.
- **A debt called in.** A creditor wants its boon repaid before a deadline.
- **Inquiry.** The Sheriff wants the vampire who caused too much breach.
- **A blood hunt.** A court invites any Kindred to destroy someone, as a mission.
- **The archon arrives.** An inspection, after you petition to become a domain.
- **The Pyramid's tithe.** Once a year, for each Tremere.

## Clan warnings

A Malkavian colony gets warned of some threats before they arrive, through the clan's insight or
a dream (and now and then a false alarm). See [Clans](Clans.md#weaknesses-and-callings).
