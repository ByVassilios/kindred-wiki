# Character sheet

Under the hood the mod keeps a Vampire: The Masquerade character sheet for every Kindred. You
never roll dice yourself. You see the results in messages and in the roll log (Kindred tab).

## Dice

A roll is a handful of ten-sided dice, usually an Attribute plus an Ability. Each die at or above
the difficulty (often 6) is a success. Each 1 cancels a success. No successes and at least one 1
is a botch, which is worse than a plain failure.

Dice decide Discipline powers and resisting them, frenzy, sins, rituals, hunting trips and
witnesses noticing things. Ordinary shooting and melee stay on RimWorld's own rules.

## Attributes

Nine Attributes, rated 1 to 5 (higher for low generations). They're worked out from the pawn as
it is right now, so they follow whatever happens to it.

| Attribute | Comes from |
|---|---|
| Strength | Melee, manipulation, body size, Tough, Brawler |
| Dexterity | Shooting, Melee, moving, manipulation, Nimble, Jogger |
| Stamina | Health, Tough, Wimp |
| Charisma | Social, Kind, Abrasive |
| Manipulation | Social, talking, Psychopath, Greedy |
| Appearance | Beauty |
| Perception | Sight, hearing, Shooting |
| Intelligence | Intellectual, consciousness, Too Smart, Slow Learner |
| Wits | Consciousness, Social, Intellectual, Nerves |

Dots bought with experience (Attributes menu in the Kindred tab) sit on top of that.

Each dot above 2, bought or not, also gives a vampire stats for good. Older blood gets 5% more from
each dot for every generation below the 13th, except Stamina. Hover an Attribute in the Kindred tab
to see this vampire's numbers.

| Attribute | Each dot above 2 |
|---|---|
| Strength | +8% melee damage, +5 kg carrying capacity |
| Dexterity | +2 levels of Melee for hitting and dodging |
| Stamina | 5% less damage taken |
| Charisma | +4% social impact |
| Manipulation | +4% negotiation ability |
| Perception | +1 level of Shooting for accuracy |
| Intelligence | +4% research speed |
| Wits | 4% less time spent aiming |
| Appearance | Nothing; it only rolls |

## Abilities

Abilities are read from RimWorld skills: skill level ÷ 4, up to 5. Firearms is Shooting, Medicine
is Medicine, the social Abilities are Social, and so on. Two are the mod's own and are raised with
experience: **Stealth** and **Occult**.

## Virtues

| Virtue | Used for | Path version |
|---|---|---|
| Conscience | Sin rolls | Conviction |
| Self-Control | Resisting frenzy | Instinct |
| Courage | Resisting Rötschreck | Courage |

Rolled at the Embrace from traits: kind pawns get more Conscience, iron-willed ones more
Self-Control, tough ones more Courage.

## Willpower

Willpower starts equal to Courage.

- **Spending it:** one automatic success on a roll, a moment of control in a frenzy, a day free of
  a derangement or clan compulsion, and the cost of some powers.
- **Getting it back:** a full, safe daysleep, and a good-mood day.
- **Policy.** Each vampire spends Willpower by itself according to its policy: never, to resist
  frenzy (the default), or freely.

## Age

| Age | Years undead |
|---|---|
| Neonate | Under 50 |
| Ancilla | 50 to 200 |
| Elder | Over 200 |

A vampire Embraced in play stays a neonate for any normal game. Older vampires are met,
recruited or dug up. The old sleep deeper by day and rise faster at court.

## Experience

Kindred earn experience into a pool and spend it in the Kindred tab.

**Earning:** a little each night, and more for ambitions, Path deeds, lessons, fights, missions,
speeches and more. Every source is on the [Experience](Experience.md) page. The rate is a mod
setting.

**Spending:**

| What | Cost |
|---|---|
| New Discipline | 10 (15 self-taught) |
| In-clan Discipline | Current dots × 5 |
| Out-of-clan Discipline | Current × 7 (× 10 self-taught) |
| Any Discipline, Caitiff | Current × 6 |
| New sorcery path | 7 |
| Secondary sorcery path | Current × 4 |
| Attribute | Current × 4 |
| Stealth or Occult | 3 new, then current × 2 |
| Virtue | Current × 2 |
| Humanity or Path | Current × 2 |
| Willpower | Current rating |

Out-of-clan Disciplines need a teacher, except Celerity, Fortitude and Potence, which can be
self-taught. Three [lessons](Experience.md#lessons) from a colony vampire who knows it open one.
