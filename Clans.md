# Clans

Every vampire belongs to the clan of its sire. The clan decides three in-clan Disciplines (cheaper
to learn), a **weakness**, and a **calling**: one thing the clan is good for in a colony.

Weaknesses are full strength by default. The "soften clan weaknesses" setting gives each a milder
form.

## The thirteen clans and the Caitiff

| Clan | Disciplines | Sect | In short |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Brujah** | Celerity, Potence, Presence | Camarilla | Rebels and firebrands, quick to anger and quicker to act. They argue for every cause and fight for most of them. |
| **Gangrel** | Animalism, Fortitude, Protean | Camarilla | Loners and wanderers, closer to the Beast than other Kindred. Animals accept them, and each frenzy leaves its mark. |
| **Nosferatu** | Animalism, Obfuscate, Potence | Camarilla | Twisted into monsters by the Embrace. They live hidden, trade in secrets and hear everything first. |
| **Toreador** | Auspex, Celerity, Presence | Camarilla | Artists and patrons who chase beauty, sometimes to the point of losing themselves in it. |
| **Ventrue** | Dominate, Fortitude, Presence | Camarilla | Rulers by habit and by right, or so they say. Each can feed on only one kind of mortal. |
| **Assamite** | Celerity, Obfuscate, Quietus | Independent | Assassins from the mountain fortress of Alamut, bound by an old curse that makes other Kindred's blood burn them. |
| **Followers of Set** | Obfuscate, Presence, Serpentis | Independent | Priests of a dark god who deal in vice and secrets. The sun hates them more than most, and bright light dazzles them. |
| **Lasombra** | Dominate, Obtenebration, Potence | Sabbat | Proud masters of shadow who cast no reflection. Born to lead, or so they tell everyone. |
| **Malkavian** | Auspex, Dementation, Obfuscate | Camarilla | Every one of them is mad, and some see more clearly for it. Their warnings make sense afterwards, mostly. |
| **Ravnos** | Animalism, Chimerstry, Fortitude | Independent | Wanderers, tricksters and illusionists, each in the grip of one vice they can rarely refuse. |
| **Tzimisce** | Animalism, Auspex, Vicissitude | Sabbat | Old lords of the eastern mountains and masters of flesh. They must sleep near earth from home or lose themselves night by night. |
| **Tremere** | Auspex, Dominate, Thaumaturgy | Camarilla | Wizards who stole immortality and made blood magic of it. They answer to a strict pyramid of elders and bond to them more easily than other Kindred. |
| **Giovanni** | Dominate, Necromancy, Potence | Independent | A Venetian family of merchants and necromancers who Embrace their own relatives. Their bite brings agony instead of pleasure. |
| **Caitiff** | None in-clan | Any | Vampires with no clan. Their blood carries no clan's gifts and no clan's curse, and no clan will claim them. |

## Weaknesses and callings

| Clan | Weakness | Calling |
|---|---|---|
| Assamite | Other Kindred's blood burns them instead of feeding them, and they can't commit diablerie | **Contracts.** Assassination missions only they can take, paid in silver and boons |
| Brujah | Frenzy is harder to resist, and Willpower can't stop it | **Firebrand.** Wins mortals round and recruits prisoners faster |
| Followers of Set | Sunlight hurts more, and bright light costs a die on every roll and slows work | **Tempter.** Drugs given to outsiders make them strings, quickly |
| Gangrel | Each frenzy leaves an animal feature: fur, eyes, claws, ears, skittishness. Some become permanent | **Wanderer.** A caravan with a Gangrel feeds its Kindred from game |
| Giovanni | Their bite hurts: no Kiss, a scream that wakes sleepers, twice the blood loss | **Family business.** Better trade prices, and they can sell corpses |
| Lasombra | No reflection. Mortals near mirrors or still water may notice | **Kingmaker.** Whoever they back at court rises faster |
| Malkavian | One derangement that can never be cured | **Insight.** Cryptic warnings before some threats arrive. Sometimes wrong |
| Nosferatu | Hideous. Any outsider who sees one undisguised is a witness | **Secrets.** Hears court news first and can sell secrets for boons |
| Ravnos | A vice (theft, deceit or cruelty) they act on when the chance comes, unless they pass a roll | **Swindler.** Once per trade visit, one much better deal. Sometimes caught |
| Toreador | Excellent art can leave them standing entranced | **Patron.** Their art earns standing at court and at Elysium |
| Tremere | Blood bonds take hold of them one step faster | **The Pyramid.** A yearly tithe; in return, rituals and cheaper Thaumaturgy |
| Tzimisce | Must sleep near earth from home, or every pool halves night after night | **Fleshcrafter.** Better surgery |
| Ventrue | Can drink from only one kind of mortal (men, the old, soldiers, prisoners and so on) | **Rule.** Holds more strings on mortals, and they're found out less |
| Caitiff | No weakness, but no clan: every Discipline costs a bit more, and sect Kindred scorn them | None |

Compulsions (Toreador, Ravnos, Malkavian) never fire while the vampire is drafted, fighting or
fleeing, and come at most every few days.

### Tzimisce native soil

A Tzimisce (Old Clan Tzimisce too) needs earth from home nearby when they sleep. Home is the map
they were Embraced on. If they arrived already a vampire, it's the map they were on when the
game first checked them.

1. On that map, build a crafting spot and add the **make sack of earth** bill. It takes only
   work, no materials.
2. Put the sack in the room where the Tzimisce sleeps (it has to be indoors), or have them carry
   it in their inventory.

The check happens after each full daysleep. Each day spent without the soil adds a step of
**far from home soil**, up to four: every dice pool halves again and work gets worse. One
daysleep beside the soil clears it. "Soften clan weaknesses" in the settings stops it at the
first step.

Each sack remembers where it was dug, and its inspect pane names the place. A sack from any other
map doesn't count. The Urn of the Old Country, a Tzimisce relic, counts as native soil anywhere
on the map it's on.

Each clan has an inspiration of its own, three times as likely as a vanilla one for its members:

| Clan | Inspiration | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Assamite | The hunter's patience | Sneakier hunting, faster melee |
| Brujah | Righteous fury | Melee hits harder and faster |
| Followers of Set | The tempter's tongue | Social impact up, drugs sell for more |
| Gangrel | Call of the wild | Faster movement, better taming and training |
| Giovanni | The family business | Better trade prices |
| Lasombra | The shadows' favour | Faster, more accurate shooting |
| Malkavian | Strange clarity | Research much faster |
| Nosferatu | Ear to the walls | Better negotiation and prices |
| Ravnos | Wanderer's luck | Faster movement, carries more |
| Toreador | Rapture of beauty | Social impact and beauty up |
| Tremere | Hunger for knowledge | Learns and reads much faster |
| Tzimisce | The flesh obeys | Better surgery and tending |
| Ventrue | Born to rule | Better negotiation and arrests |

### Clan grudges

Kindred bring old grudges into the colony: a small opinion modifier between two vampires.
Everyone likes their own clan a little. Tzimisce and Tremere hate each other most. Brujah and
Ventrue, Gangrel and Ravnos, and Toreador and Nosferatu don't get along. Most clans are a little
wary of the Tremere, Setites, Ravnos, Giovanni and Caitiff. A blood bond or the sire-childe tie
easily outweighs any of this.

## Bloodlines

Rare lines with their own Disciplines and weaknesses. They turn up among NPCs, sleepers and
raiders, and you can pick them in the making menu.

| Clan | Disciplines | Sect | In short |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Samedi** | Fortitude, Obfuscate, Thanatosis | None | Walking corpses from the Caribbean who work as killers for hire. Their flesh rots and sloughs and grows back, and they smell like it. |
| **Harbingers of Skulls** | Auspex, Fortitude, Necromancy | Sabbat | Shrunken, grinning corpses who say they were dead for five hundred years. All of them are elders, all of them are Sabbat, and they know the dead better than the Giovanni do. |
| **Nagaraja** | Auspex, Dominate, Necromancy | None | Flesh-eating necromancers, nearly hunted out of existence. They have jagged teeth in place of fangs, need flesh as well as blood, and tear it with every feeding. |
| **True Brujah** | Potence, Presence, Temporis | None | Cold, patient scholars who say they are the real heirs of Brujah, robbed by Troile. They feel less with every year, and time itself bends for them. |
| **Salubri** | Auspex, Fortitude, Obeah | None | Healers with a third eye, hunted since the Tremere drank their founder. Only a handful are left, seeking Golconda, and taking blood from the unwilling costs them dearly. |
| **Daughters of Cacophony** | Fortitude, Melpominee, Presence | None | Singers, nearly all of them women, who hear music every moment of the night. Their songs can lift a crowd, drive a listener mad or speak in an ear miles away. The music in their heads makes them a little deaf to everything else. |
| **Baali** | Daimoinon, Obfuscate, Presence | None | Infernalists who believe the world is doomed and work to hurry it along. Every other Kindred wants them destroyed. They can't bear holy things, and True Faith hurts them twice over. |
| **Gargoyles** | Flight, Fortitude, Potence, Visceratika | Camarilla | Grey, winged things made by the Tremere out of other Kindred to be guards and slaves. Many have broken free and joined the Camarilla. None can pass for human, and their minds were made to obey. |
| **Blood Brothers** | Fortitude, Potence, Sanguinus | Sabbat | Sabbat shock troops made by sorcery out of kidnapped mortals, stripped of their selves and given matching faces. A circle of them shares one mind and one pain. They can't make childer: an Embrace only kills. |

| Line | Weakness |
|---|---|
| Baali | Holy symbols and True Faith hurt them twice as much. Every clan hates them |
| Blood Brothers | Can't Embrace (the mortal dies). They share each other's pain |
| Daughters of Cacophony | Music always in their ears: worse hearing and sight |
| Gargoyles | Hideous and weak-willed against mind control. Made by a Tremere rite |
| Harbingers of Skulls | Corpse-like and hideous |
| Kiasyd | Iron (steel) weapons burn them and can send them into frenzy |
| Nagaraja | Need flesh as well as blood: their feeding tears wounds, and they weaken without it |
| Salubri | Feeding on an unwilling vessel costs Willpower |
| Samedi | Rotting and hideous |
| True Brujah | Conscience rolls are harder, and Virtues and Humanity cost double experience |

## Lost bloodlines

These lines are gone from the world. You'll only meet them as sleepers in ancient caskets and in
torpor crypts, but they join and Embrace like anyone else.

| Clan | Disciplines | Sect | In short |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tremere antitribu** | Auspex, Dominate, Thaumaturgy | Sabbat | Tremere who went over to the Sabbat and were marked as traitors. One night every one of them burned. Only those asleep somewhere far away might be left. |
| **Ahrimanes** | Animalism, Presence, Spiritus | None | Gangrel women remade by a shaman's working into something that speaks with spirits. Their blood is dead: it makes no childer, no ghouls and no bonds. The line vanished in the 1990s. |
| **Anda** | Animalism, Fortitude, Protean | None | Mongol warriors of the Gangrel blood who rode with the hordes and guarded their tribes. They can't stay put: a few days sleeping in one place and they begin to fail. None have been seen since the fall of the Khans. |
| **Cappadocians** | Auspex, Fortitude, Necromancy | None | The clan of death-scholars that the Giovanni swallowed whole in the fifteenth century. Pale as corpses, and paler with age. A few may still lie asleep somewhere the Giovanni never found. |
| **Lamia** | Fortitude, Necromancy, Potence | None | The Cappadocians' guardians, women devoted to the Dark Mother, who died defending their masters from the Giovanni. Their bite carries a plague. |
| **Noiad** | Animalism, Auspex, Protean | None | Gangrel shamans of the far north who read the will of the gods in the northern lights and guarded their tribe jealously. They can't feed on animals at all. |
| **Telyavelic Tremere** | Auspex, Presence, Thaumaturgy | None | Tremere who went to pagan Lithuania, were taken for gods of the dead and came to believe it. Christian faith frightens them as it did their flock. The clan says they were wiped out in the sixteenth century. |
| **Lhiannan** | Animalism, Ogham, Presence | None | Pagan witches of the British forests, each carrying a shard of an old forest spirit. They are bound to their own woods and grow uneasy away from them, and mortals feel there is something wrong about them. The Inquisition burned the last of them, or so it is said. |

## The Children of Osiris

The Children of Osiris aren't a clan. A sleeper of any clan but the Followers of Set may be one:
it keeps its clan, and gains Bardo as an in-clan Discipline. It must stay on Humanity.
