# Humanity and the Paths

Every vampire holds to a morality: Humanity, or one of the Paths of Enlightenment. Its rating,
from 10 down to 0, sets how long torpor lasts and the most dice a vampire can roll by day. Each
morality has its own list of sins. A sin counts when its rating is at or below the vampire's
current rating: then it rolls a Virtue, and a failed roll costs a point. The further a vampire
falls, the fewer sins still trouble it. How it all works is on
[Humanity and frenzy](Humanity-and-Frenzy.md).

On a Path, Conviction can take the place of Conscience and Instinct the place of Self-Control.
Conviction is rolled after a sin against the Path. Instinct is rolled when something provokes a
frenzy, and lets the vampire ride it with some control (see
[riding the wave](Humanity-and-Frenzy.md#paths-of-enlightenment)).

To take up a Path a vampire has to fall to Humanity 3 or lower first. A follower of a suitable
Path may then come to the colony at night and offer to teach; after a season of study comes the
test.

In the tables, "8" means a vampire at 8 or higher feels that sin. One that has fallen to 7 or
lower no longer does.

### Humanity

The human conscience the vampire brought with it from life, worn down a little by every sin.

*Virtues:* Conscience, Self-Control, Courage.

| Rating | Sin |
|---|---|
| 8 | Fed on someone against their will |
| 8 | Hurt someone who meant no harm |
| 8 | Forced blood on someone who didn't want it |
| 6 | Killed someone who meant no harm |
| 6 | Killed a vessel by drinking it dry |
| 4 | Killed someone who was already down |
| 4 | Killed someone in a frenzy |
| 4 | Hurt a captive for answers |
| 3 | Executed a prisoner |
| 3 | Drained a vessel dry on purpose |
| 3 | Forced the Embrace on someone who didn't want it |
| 1 | Devoured another vampire's soul |

### Path of Blood

A code of the blood: other Kindred are to be converted or destroyed, and killing mortals for food is beneath a true follower.

*Virtues:* Conviction, Self-Control, Courage. *Most often followed by:* Assamite.

| Rating | Sin |
|---|---|
| 10 | Killed a vessel by drinking it dry |
| 6 | Lost control to a frenzy |
| 1 | Destroyed a vampire of its own clan |

### Path of the Bones

Death is the one subject worth studying. Its followers fear nothing about it, waste no chance to watch it, and never stand in its way.

*Virtues:* Conviction, Self-Control, Courage. *Most often followed by:* Giovanni.

| Rating | Sin |
|---|---|
| 10 | Fled in terror from fire or sunlight |
| 8 | Killed someone who meant no harm |
| 7 | Gone hungry when it could have fed |
| 6 | Lost control to a frenzy |
| 5 | Taken someone captive instead of killing them |
| 2 | Tended someone else's wounds |

### Path of Caine

Vampires are the children of Caine and should live as what they are: apart from mortals, masters of the Beast, never denying their own hunger.

*Virtues:* Conviction, Instinct, Courage.

| Rating | Sin |
|---|---|
| 6 | Lost control to a frenzy |
| 5 | Fled in terror from fire or sunlight |
| 1 | Gone hungry when it could have fed |

### Path of Cathari

Followers indulge every appetite, let the Beast run with them, and kill only when passion takes them.

*Virtues:* Conviction, Instinct, Courage.

| Rating | Sin |
|---|---|
| 10 | Held back a frenzy |
| 7 | Lost control to a frenzy |
| 5 | Killed someone in a frenzy |
| 2 | Killed someone who meant no harm |

### Path of the Feral Heart

The vampire is a predator and should live like one: hunting with its own body, feeding when hungry, killing cleanly and never for sport.

*Virtues:* Conviction, Instinct, Courage. *Most often followed by:* Gangrel.

| Rating | Sin |
|---|---|
| 10 | Killed with a weapon instead of its own body |
| 8 | Stood in the sun |
| 7 | Killed someone who was already down |
| 7 | Executed a prisoner |
| 6 | Gone hungry when it could have fed |
| 4 | Killed someone who meant no harm |
| 3 | Held back a frenzy |

### Path of Honorable Accord

A knightly code of duty, loyalty and keeping one's word. Its followers stand their ground and never kill without reason.

*Virtues:* Conscience, Self-Control, Courage.

| Rating | Sin |
|---|---|
| 3 | Fled in terror from fire or sunlight |
| 2 | Killed someone who meant no harm |
| 2 | Killed someone who was already down |

### Path of Lilith

Followers believe pain teaches, hunger most of all. They go hungry on purpose, fear nothing of death, and see every killing as a lesson cut short.

*Virtues:* Conviction, Instinct, Courage.

| Rating | Sin |
|---|---|
| 10 | Fed before the hunger had grown strong |
| 5 | Fled in terror from fire or sunlight |
| 4 | Killed someone who meant no harm |
| 4 | Killed someone who was already down |

| 4 | Killed someone in a frenzy |

### Path of Metamorphosis

The vampire is one stage in a longer change. Followers feed without delay, study the Beast by riding it, and care nothing for what others need.

*Virtues:* Conviction, Instinct, Courage. *Most often followed by:* Tzimisce.

|---|---|
