# Humanity and frenzy

Every vampire has a Beast inside it. Humanity measures how well the vampire holds it back, and a
frenzy is the Beast getting loose.

## Humanity

A rating from 0 to 10, shown in the Kindred tab. Most vampires start around 7.

### Sins

Doing something worse than your Humanity allows is a sin. The vampire rolls Conscience at
difficulty 8. Fail, and it loses a point of Humanity. Botch, and it also gains a
[derangement](#derangements). A vampire only tests for sins at or below its current rating, so
the lower it falls, the less it feels.

Every sin and its rating, for Humanity and for each Path, is on
[Humanity and the Paths](Paths-of-Enlightenment.md).

Killing raiders in an open fight is never a sin. At most one test per level per day, so one bad
night can't strip five points.

### What Humanity does

| Effect | How |
|---|---|
| Waking | Higher Humanity wakes earlier at dusk |
| Daytime | Every roll by day is capped at Humanity, and low Humanity makes a vampire slower by day |
| Torpor | Lower Humanity means longer torpor |
| Mortals | Below 5, social penalties with mortals. Below 3, corpse-like unless it looks alive |
| Animals | Below 4, animals shy away more |
| 0 | **Wassail.** The vampire is lost to the Beast for good and turns on the colony |

**Raising Humanity** costs current rating × 2 experience, and only after a quadrum with no
Humanity lost.

### The wight that got away

A vampire lost at Humanity 0 becomes hostile on the spot. Destroy it, or stake it and keep it.
If it escapes the map, it stays out there: the breach score creeps up while it's loose, and now
and then it comes back at night with animals it has ghouled, stronger each time. A court you know
may offer a mission to hunt it down.

## Paths of Enlightenment

Paths are moralities that aren't human. Each has its own list of sins and often its own Virtues:
Conviction in place of Conscience, Instinct in place of Self-Control.

| Morality | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Humanity** | The human conscience the vampire brought with it from life, worn down a little by every sin. |
| **Path of Blood** | A code of the blood: other Kindred are to be converted or destroyed, and killing mortals for food is beneath a true follower. |
| **Path of the Bones** | Death is the one subject worth studying. Its followers fear nothing about it, waste no chance to watch it, and never stand in its way. |
| **Path of Caine** | Vampires are the children of Caine and should live as what they are: apart from mortals, masters of the Beast, never denying their own hunger. |
| **Path of Cathari** | Followers indulge every appetite, let the Beast run with them, and kill only when passion takes them. |
| **Path of the Feral Heart** | The vampire is a predator and should live like one: hunting with its own body, feeding when hungry, killing cleanly and never for sport. |
| **Path of Honorable Accord** | A knightly code of duty, loyalty and keeping one's word. Its followers stand their ground and never kill without reason. |
| **Path of Lilith** | Followers believe pain teaches, hunger most of all. They go hungry on purpose, fear nothing of death, and see every killing as a lesson cut short. |
| **Path of Metamorphosis** | The vampire is one stage in a longer change. Followers feed without delay, study the Beast by riding it, and care nothing for what others need. |
| **Path of Night** | Kindred are damned to be horrors whose work is to frighten. Followers kill sparingly, rely on no one and bow to no one. |
| **Path of Paradox** | Kindred have slipped out of the great cycle and must send others back into it. Followers kill only for need and guard their own bloodline. |
| **Path of Power and the Inner Voice** | Power is the only thing worth having. Followers own their acts, reward the useful, help no one for free and never accept defeat. |
| **Path of Typhon** | Every vice is a tool for raising a sleeping god. Followers corrupt others, help their own and show no one any mercy. |

Each Path's Virtues and full list of sins are on [Humanity and the Paths](Paths-of-Enlightenment.md).

**Taking up a Path.** A vampire must first fall to Humanity 3 or lower. A follower of a Path that
suits its clan may then come to the colony at night and offer to teach. After a season of study
there's a Willpower test. Failing costs a point and the test comes again in a few days, or a
season after a botch.

**Living on a Path.** Path followers unsettle mortals (social impact down) and can't use Look
Alive. Sabbat Kindred approve.

**Instinct.** A vampire on a Path with Instinct rolls it when a frenzy threatens. If the
provocation is below its Instinct, no frenzy comes. Otherwise it may **ride the wave**: no pain, harder melee
hits, guns dropped, still under your control.

## Golconda

Golconda is a lasting peace with the Beast. A vampire who has kept its Humanity can work toward
it over a long time, and can lose it again easily.

1. **Hear of it.** From a lore text, rumours heard at a court or a sleeper who remembers. Until then the
   option doesn't show.
2. **Be eligible.** On Humanity, at 7 or more, Conscience 4 or more, no diablerie stain.
3. **Find a mentor.** The court that told you, or the first court you know, puts the mission on offer. Asking costs a little standing.
4. **Make amends.** Tending people and taking captives alive count, and so does a season without
   losing Humanity. The vampire needs 10 plus one for every Humanity point it ever lost. You never
   see the count, only a thought that hints at it.
5. **The Suspire.** A button at night. The vampire lies in a trance for about six hours, then rolls
   Conscience, Self-Control and Courage. All three must succeed.

**Failing the Suspire** costs a point of Humanity and closes Golconda for good.

**Reaching it:** frenzy and Rötschreck never take hold (unless starving, or Rötschreck while on
fire), waking costs a fifth as much blood, no daytime dice cap, and a standing good mood. Falling
below Humanity 7 or Conscience 4 loses it forever.

## Frenzy

A frenzy is a mental state. There are three kinds:

| Kind | What sets it off | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Rage** | A heavy hit, insults, a fight, any hit while badly hurt, a lover downed nearby | Attacks the source, then anything close. Melee only, ignores pain |
| **Hunger** | A vessel nearby while starving, the first taste while hungry | Brings down the nearest vessel and drinks past the safe limit |
| **Rötschreck** | Fire within a few cells, being burned, the sun, True Faith | Runs for the nearest dark, safe spot, attacking anything in the way |

- **The roll.** Self-Control for rage and hunger, Courage for Rötschreck, against a difficulty
  set by what provoked it. Three successes resist it; one or two buy a moment before another roll.
- **Willpower.** A point buys a short window of control. Each vampire's Willpower policy (under
  Habits on the Kindred tab) decides when it's spent by itself: never, against frenzy, or freely.
- **Afterwards.** Anything killed in frenzy can be a sin. Gangrel may gain an animal feature.
  Outsiders who saw it are witnesses.
- **Clans.** Brujah frenzy more easily and can't spend Willpower against it.

An alert shows when a vampire is at risk.

## Derangements

Lasting madness, gained from a botched sin roll and some powers. Malkavians always have one that
never goes away. Each causes an episode every so often. A Willpower point holds one off for a day.

| Derangement | Effect |
|---|---|
| Fugue | Wanders off in a daze |
| Paranoia | Social penalty, hides in its room |
| Hysteria | Frenzy and Rötschreck are much harder to resist |
| Manic-depression | Long mood swings |
| Megalomania | Good mood holding an office, bad mood without; insulting sprees |
| Sanguinary animism | Guilt after every feeding, hears voices |
| Obsessive-compulsive | Hates a dirty room, cleaning binges |
| Multiple personalities | Swaps to a second set of work priorities |
| Schizophrenia | Psychotic wandering, voices, frenzy a little easier |
| Bulimia | Overfeeds, then throws blood up on the floor |
