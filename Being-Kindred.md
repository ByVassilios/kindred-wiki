# Being Kindred

A vampire keeps its skills, traits, relationships and backstory. Most of what keeps a colonist
alive stops mattering, and a few new things start to.

## What changes

| Gone | New |
|---|---|
| Food and Rest needs | The **Vitae** need: the blood pool |
| Ageing, disease, toxic buildup, breathing, bleeding out | Burns in sunlight and takes fire badly |
| Natural healing and medical tending | Heals only by spending blood |
| Most pain | Sleeps by day whether it likes it or not |
| Losing a lung, kidney, liver or stomach | Can be staked through the heart |

Psychic drones, soothes and other psychic effects still work on Kindred.

## The blood pool

Blood (vitae) pays for everything: waking up each night, healing, the blood buff and most
Discipline powers.

- **Upkeep.** One point each time the vampire wakes. With an empty pool it can't wake and falls
  into torpor.
- **Hunger.** Above 70% the vampire is sated. Below 40% it's hungry, and below 15% it's starving
  and may frenzy at the sight of a vessel.
- **Spend rate.** Generation limits how much blood a vampire can spend in a short burst. A big
  power is paid partly now and the rest as the limit refills.

### Generation

Lower generation means closer to Caine: a bigger pool, a faster spend rate, and higher limits on
Attributes and Disciplines.

| Generation | Blood pool | Per turn | Highest dot |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4th | 50 | 10 | 9 |
| 5th | 40 | 8 | 8 |
| 6th | 30 | 6 | 7 |
| 7th | 20 | 4 | 6 |
| 8th | 15 | 3 | 5 |
| 9th | 14 | 2 | 5 |
| 10th | 13 | 1 | 5 |
| 11th | 12 | 1 | 5 |
| 12th | 11 | 1 | 5 |
| 13th | 10 | 1 | 5 |

The 14th and 15th generations are **thin-bloods**: a smaller usable pool, a lower cap on
Disciplines, and every blood cost but upkeep doubled. In return the sun hurts them less and they
can keep food down. A 13th-generation vampire's childer are 14th.

Animal blood is worth half from the 8th generation down and nothing from
the 6th. The only way to lower your generation in play is [diablerie](Embrace-Bonds-and-Ghouls.md#diablerie).

## Healing

Healing is automatic. Each vampire's heal policy (never, when safe, always) under Habits on the
Kindred tab decides when it spends blood on it.

- **Ordinary wounds.** One blood point heals 12 HP of injury, worst wounds first.
- **Aggravated wounds** (fire, sunlight, fangs and claws, some powers): 5 points and a full
  daysleep heal 10 HP. They are what kills a vampire for good.
- **Lost limbs** grow back through blood, at the aggravated cost.

## Damage

- **Blunt** damage is halved.
- **Bullets and arrows** do 70% damage.
- **Melee blades** do full damage.
- **Burns** of any kind, from any mod, are aggravated.
- However armour, Fortitude and the rest stack, damage never drops below 40%, so nothing makes a vampire
  untouchable.

## Sunlight

Sunlight deals aggravated burns every second the vampire stands under an open sky by day. Full
clothing cuts the damage to about a third but never stops it. A neonate at noon has well under a
minute (real time, at 1x speed). A thin-blood gets an ordinary **sunburn** instead, which heals
like any wound.

- Dawn and dusk hurt much less than noon. Eclipses and dark weather help too.
- Stepping into the sun can trigger **Rötschreck**, a panic that sends the vampire running for
  shade. See [Humanity and frenzy](Humanity-and-Frenzy.md#frenzy).
- An alert warns you about Kindred outdoors two hours before dawn. Vampires head for a roof on
  their own when the sky starts to brighten.

## Fire

Fire is the other thing vampires fear. Burns are aggravated, Kindred catch fire more easily, and
fire nearby can trigger Rötschreck. Firefighting is off for Kindred by default.

## Daysleep

From dawn to dusk a vampire sleeps in a bed, a coffin, or any roofed cell.

- **The coffin** is a bed anyone can sleep in. Its lid keeps the sun off even under an open
  sky, and a full day in it is a good mood.
- **Woken by day** (drafted, or by danger), a vampire gets **day lethargy**: slow and clumsy, more so at low
  Humanity, and its rolls are capped at its Humanity.
- **Waking time.** High Humanity wakes earlier at dusk.
- **Eclipses.** When an eclipse or a dark sky leaves the day too dim to burn, vampires wake
  as at night: no daysleep, no lethargy and no cap on their rolls. Dawn and dusk don't count, and
  hunters and raids still treat it as day.
- **Dug in.** A vampire caught with no roof in reach digs into soft ground and sleeps there.
  It's a bad day's sleep for anyone but a Gangrel, and outsiders who see it dug up start
  talking.
- **Dreams.** After a full daysleep a vampire sometimes dreams: of its old life, its sire, the
  Beast, or, for a Malkavian, of something about to happen.

## The night shift

- Kindred take no penalty for darkness and never get the "in darkness" thought.
- They won't start outdoor work by day. Drafted orders still go through.
- To keep mortals awake at the same hours, copy a vampire's schedule to them in the Schedule tab.
- A mortal partner doesn't mind sleeping apart.

## Torpor

When a vampire would die from anything but aggravated damage, or wakes with an empty pool, it
falls into torpor: down, unconscious, no upkeep. Wounds don't heal, but lost parts grow back.

- **How long** depends on Humanity: about a day at 10, years at 1. A letter gives the expected
  date.
- **Starved into torpor**, a vampire lies there until someone gives it 4 blood points (vials or
  another vampire's vitae). It wakes in a hunger frenzy.
- **Ending it early.** Vitae from a vampire at least two generations lower wakes it.
- **Torn half apart.** A vampire that goes down with about half its body gone falls into torpor
  as well. While torpid it regrows one lost part every three hours, as long as it has more than
  2 blood. A healing policy of "never" stops this.
- **Choosing it.** A button on the vampire puts it into torpor to sit out a bad stretch.
- A torpid vampire can be carried, kept in a coffin, staked, or diablerised.

## Final Death

A vampire is destroyed for good when:

- its head is destroyed or cut off;
- its body is destroyed (burned up, eaten);
- aggravated damage hits it while it's in torpor or down;
- sunlight or fire takes it past the point of death;
- someone completes diablerie on it.

What's left depends on its true age: a young vampire leaves a corpse, an old one leaves ash.

## Staking

A wooden stake through the heart paralyses a vampire. It stays awake and helpless until the
stake is pulled. A stake can be wielded as a weapon, and the stake crossbow shoots them: a hit that
lands in the heart pins the vampire about half the time. Right-click a downed or torpid vampire to drive one in
where it lies, for certain. It takes a moment in torpor and longer while the vampire is awake and struggling. A pawn with no heart can't be staked.

## In a fight

- **Fangs** show when a vampire is drafted, fighting, feeding or in frenzy.
- **Moving as a cloud.** A vampire with three or more dots in a Discipline moves in a faint cloud
  of that Discipline's colour when drafted or charging, faster than it walks.
- **Physical Disciplines show.** Celerity dashes, Potence knocks foes back, and a dodge is a
  sidestep or a clash of blows.
- **Stuns** hit a vampire less the lower its generation is compared with whoever stunned it.
- **Crawling.** A vampire that loses its legs and still has blood keeps fighting from the ground
  instead of going down.
- **Gore.** Blows spray blood, and a part cut off throws what butchering it would give. A
  Gargoyle's throws stone, and an old vampire's turns to ash. A kill with Potence 5 or Taste of
  Death bursts the body. Close kills, feeding frenzies and diablerie leave the vampire covered in
  blood. The amount of gore and the fighting motion are mod settings.

## Eating for show

From the right-click menu a vampire can eat a meal to look human. It costs a Willpower point
(free with the Eat Food Merit, or for a thin-blood). Unless it's a thin-blood, it has to throw
up a couple of hours later, and it waits until no outsider is looking if it can.

## Looking alive

Every vampire has a power to pass as alive until dawn: warm skin, breath, a pulse. It removes
the corpse-like social penalty with mortals. It costs 8 blood minus the vampire's Humanity, so
it's free at 8 and above. A vampire on a Path can't use it.
