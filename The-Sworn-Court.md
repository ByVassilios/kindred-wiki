# The sworn court

Pledge the colony to one court and its Kindred stop being names on a list. Each has a personality, means, a goal of
its own and an opinion of you that moves with what you do. They hand you work, owe you boons, fight beside you and,
if they come to hate you, work against you.

## Pledging

Open a court from the Courts page of the Kindred window (or visit it) and use **Swear to this home** on its Court
page. You need:

- a colony vampire Acknowledged at that court,
- the court not shunning, banishing or blood-hunting the colony.

One court at a time. Pledging doesn't stop you joining a coterie there. The court's roster fills to 8 to 12 named
members, and new pages appear on the Kindred window's rail: **People**, **Council** and, while a war runs, **War**.

Leaving costs 20 Standing. Loyal members drop to -5 regard, and the boons the court's members owe you shrink a size
(trivial ones are gone). A court that falls or changes hands frees the colony with a letter.

## People

The People page lists every named member: seat, clan and regard, with a filter for friends, rivals and enemies.
Click a member to see their personality, means, goal, friends and dislikes. You only see a member's personality
and means once you've **met** them: talked to them in a town, finished a mission they gave, or read them with Aura
Perception.

| Regard event | Change |
|---|---|
| A mission done for them | +2 |
| A mission done for their coterie or a friend | +1 |
| A step of their goal done | +3 |
| A gift from their row | +1 per 500 silver, up to +3 a season |
| Their mission refused or let lapse | -1 (Proud -3) |
| A mission done against them or a friend | -3 |
| Betraying their goal | -10, for good |

Regard drifts a point toward 0 every season.

### Personalities

| Personality | How they pay and behave |
|---|---|
| Greedy | silver x0.7, Standing x1.5 |
| Cautious | silver and Standing x1.25, but the biggest boon they'll owe is a size smaller |
| Loyal | everything x1.25 while you never cross them or their friends; betrayal makes an enemy for good |
| Conniving | pays with a boon another member owes them |
| Cowardly | one more open mission, aimed at their rivals; pay x1.2 |
| Proud | Standing x1.5, silver x0.8; refusals cost 3 regard |
| Zealous | only the court's cause; those pay x1.5 |
| Generous | everything x1.3; now and then asks a favour back |
| Vengeful | bad blood never fades; an enemy at -5 |
| Decadent | pays in blood vials, rarely silver |
| Paranoid | regard grows half as fast; tells you what it wants only if you have a hold over it |

A coterie's eldest has the personality of its temperament: Tyrant Proud, Scholar Cautious, Trickster Conniving,
Traditionalist Loyal, Schemer Paranoid, Zealot Zealous. Coterie-mates are friends.

### Means

Means come from the member's seat, wealth and the boons owed to them. A poor member offers one mission at a time and
pays x0.6, a comfortable one two at x1.0, a rich one three at x1.4. Raiding a member's asset in a war costs them a
point of wealth for a year.

### Goals

Each member wants something, hidden until they count you a friend (regard +6) or you have a hold over them: a
secret, or a major boon they owe you. A goal is three steps:
two missions bent to it, then the prize. At the end a letter asks whether you hand the prize over or keep it.
Keeping it is a betrayal.

| Goal | What you get for it |
|---|---|
| Collector | a relic |
| Lore hunter | a named tome: Saulot's travelogue, a book of Lasombra poetry or the Ravnos Chronicle |
| Power seeker | silver by its means, and its vote for a year |
| Revenge | a major boon and the enemy's goods |
| Diablerist | a major boon and a secret on another member |
| Freedom | its regard stays at +6 or more for good |
| Childe | a minor boon now, its childe on your side later |
| Golconda | each colony vampire learns, about 6 experience |
| Kingmaker | the new Prince starts at regard +6 |
| Homecoming | a torpor crypt revealed |
| Buried secret | a life boon |

## Boons

What a member owes you, or you owe them, is a boon in the ledger on the Boons page, earned by your eldest vampire.
**Call in** on a member's row cashes one in the boons' usual way.

## Seats

Under **Your seats** on the Courts page, a colony vampire can take a seat. Officers pick Hound, Sheriff, Harpy,
Keeper or Seneschal. A vampire becomes an officer by finishing a hunt for the Prince while established at the
court with no scandal. The Primogen seat needs Primogen rank: visit the court with the vampire and use Claim the
clan's seat, which needs the court's standing at 40 and no older blood of the same clan at court. Only vampires are
offered the seats. A seat held by a member makes that member your rival, and the seat's duty for the season
arrives as soon as you take it.

| Seat | Duty each season |
|---|---|
| Hound | one hunt for thin-bloods and Caitiff made without leave |
| Sheriff | one mission against trouble |
| Harpy | attend the court's Elysium in person (a season with none asks nothing) |
| Keeper | host one Elysium at home, or finish the hosting mission the seat brings |
| Seneschal | judge one petition (a letter; Wits + Etiquette hints at the right answer) |
| Primogen | vote in the council |

A duty kept adds 3 Standing and a dot. Three missed in a row and the seat is lost, with 10 Standing, to the next in
line, who starts at regard -3. The vampire's own tab shows its seat and when the duty is due, and an alert warns two
days ahead.

## The council

Each season the Prince and Primogen vote on one or two motions: Embrace with leave or freely, war or peace, the hunt
on thin-bloods and Caitiff, the Prince's tithe. Each member votes by personality, goal and friends. A colony
Primogen votes too, and a member may offer a minor boon for your vote.

## Enemies inside

Rivals (-3 to -5) slander the colony (Standing lost if they win the roll) or stymie your next mission with their own
people. Enemies (-6) send raiders: ghouls of theirs, coming in person if you hold something of theirs. Capture one
and question them in prisoner talk to learn who sent them, then **Accuse** that member. An accusation without proof
costs 10 Standing.

## War

A war runs against the nearest court of another sect, started by the council's vote or the world. Its score starts
at 50.

1. **Incursions**, a season: two or three skirmishes, +3 or -3 each.
2. **Raids**, two seasons: attack the enemy's herd, ghoul farm, chantry, haven or Elysium hall, and defend your own.
3. **The end**: at 70 or more, the final assault on their city; at 30 or less, the desperate defence of yours;
   otherwise a truce at year's end.

Victory pays silver and one of their relics, and their court falls. Defeat makes your court fall. If three or more
members survive, a letter offers to found it anew.

The skirmishes come a few weeks apart. A war mission you let lapse is fought without you: the court's members against the enemy's, by their strength, and members can die in it. Hover over the stages, the score and the holdings on the War page for what each one needs.

## Settings

Under World on the Kindred settings page: the sworn court on or off, the war chance, enemy acts a season and how
fast regard fades. Dev mode has every step under "The Rim by Night: Spotlight".
